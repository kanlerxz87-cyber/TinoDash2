Real purchasing power, indexed to 100 at transfer. Monthly compounding. Stocks enter immediately or after the selected Treasury holding period, then in equal monthly fractions over 36 months.

Immediate stocks Treasury then phased stocks

What this does—and does not—show

The hypothetical annual stock returns are: depression-like −25%, −35%, −20%, 15%, 10%, then 6%; recession-like −35%, 25%, 15%, then 6%; steady 6%. The model repeats each annual return monthly and holds the selected Treasury yield constant. Future rates are unknown. No taxes, fees, coupons, sale costs, estate cash, other assets or property cash flows are included. A proper reinvestment-rate sensitivity must separately vary future auction rates. No percentile or probability is implied.
