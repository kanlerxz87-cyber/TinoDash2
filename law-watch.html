ENACTED vs PROPOSED · OFFICIAL SOURCES ONLY

What the law says today, and what is only being talked about.

A 2040–2050 transfer will be governed by law that does not exist yet. The useful discipline is to keep a clean, sourced list of what is in force now, a separate list of open proposals, and a dated calendar for reading the authorities again.

Rule for this page: a proposal is never modelled as law. Enacted rules carry an effective date and an official citation; open proposals are listed separately with the official status page so the distinction cannot be lost.

04 · METHOD

How a law change enters the model

A repeatable five-step change procedure, so a new statute cannot quietly invalidate a page.

  1. Read the enrolled text or official guidance, not a summary article.
  2. Record the effective date and the exact transfer or tax year it first applies to.
  3. Change the anchor in law-watch and in any page that repeats it; do not leave a stale figure in place.
  4. Re-run python3 scripts/verify_sources.py --online, then rebuild the replay and the site.
  5. Note in the decision journal whether the ranking of the options moved.

Primary entry points: IRS · California Legislative Information · California State Board of Equalization · California Franchise Tax Board · Santa Clara County.
