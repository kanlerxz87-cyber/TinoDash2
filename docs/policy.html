The household signs these criteria now, before seeing any projection harden into preference. Once signed, the answer is an output of the rules — not a verdict reached by glancing at one attractive compounding chart. Edit nothing here without a dated journal entry.

The anti-reverse-engineering clause. “We will not adjust assumptions until the model says what we already want to hear.” Specifically banned: tweaking equity CAGR upward after seeing the sell-path headline; pretending the 1929 ladder is optional after a drawdown has started; excluding Prop 19 cost because it is “probably fine”; omitting Form 706 exposure because the number is uncomfortable.

Part 2 — Investment Policy Statement (IPS) for the proceeds

IPS-A · Scope

Applies to net proceeds from Property A’s sale and to any insurance proceeds designated by the trust for beneficiaries. Does not apply to Property B (held directly), retirement accounts (own custodial rules), or an emergency fund (≥ 12 months expenses in insured CDs/money market).

IPS-B · Objectives & constraints

  • Return target: maximize long-horizon real (after-inflation) return consistent with sleep-at-night constraint; benchmark = 70% Russell 3000 / 20% MSCI EAFE / 10% Bloomberg US Aggregate over full cycles; success = tracking benchmark at < 0.25% annual tracking error (index implementation).
  • Risk: equity σ ≈ 16–22% tolerable after staging completes; zero leverage; no options/writing; no crypto > 2%; no single name > 5% of portfolio; no private placements without household vote.
  • Liquidity: months 0–36 = 100% T-bills/Treasury money market (state-tax-free); months 1–36 phase-in equal monthly purchases (T+0 discipline, first business day); ≥ 24 months of family expenses always withdrawable within 7 days.
  • Time horizon: 40–60 years (spans to next generation); decumulation rules deliberately not designed here — next IPS generation at age-65 trigger.
  • Tax location: taxable brokerage; index ETFs/mutual funds with buy-and-hold; qualified dividends preferred; municipal bond sleeve only if CA top-bracket persists; loss-harvest annually (Dec); never trade around DOD date rules.
  • Concentration: household already carries residential real estate (B + maybe A) — equity sleeve stays diversified; no new real-estate concentration without vote.

IPS-D · Costs, custody, control

  • Total fund fees < 0.10%/yr; adviser fee 0.00–0.25%; TreasuryDirect for direct bills (no custodial fee).
  • Two signatures (household co-trustees) for disbursements > $100,000; journal entry same week; no discretionary single-signer moves.
  • No securities-based lending; no margin; no pledge agreements with anyone for any reason.
  • Estate documents updated to name the account explicitly (pour-over vs direct beneficiary — attorney decides; both recorded in process file).
