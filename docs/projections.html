Capital-gains sensitivity — post-inheritance appreciation (the real “sell later” tax)

Step-up erases the pre-death gain. Only appreciation after date of death is taxable. Worked example: A inherited at $5,368,200 (2040); sold 10 years later at $7,946,000 (4%/yr): gain ≈ $2,578,000. Federal: 0% to ≈ $98,900 (MFJ 2026), 15% to ≈ $613,700, 20% above → ≈ $470,000 + 3.8% NIIT ≈ $98,000. CA: no preferential rate — 12.3% on the stacked gain + 1% MHSA (Form 540 line 62, ($2,577,800 − $1,000,000) × 1% ≈ $15,780) → ≈ $332,850. Combined ≈ $900,800 ≈ 35% of the appreciation — the quantified cost of “wait too long to sell.” §121 (live 2+ yrs) would shelter $250,000/$500,000 of it. Selling at the inheritance year costs ≈ $0 on the same property.
