Process · What actually happens, in order, with clocks

The inheritance, step by step — and the deadlines that bite

Property A arrives via trust around 2040–2050. This is the full sequence — from the phone call to post-distribution annual upkeep — with every statutory clock flagged red. California administers death transfers at the county assessor level; federal at the IRS; both move on their rails whether or not the family is ready.

Clocks assume date of death (“DOD”) = Day 0. Alternate-valuation (6-month) election only if it lowers both value and estate tax — never for a gain-asset like A. “Same tax year as inheritance” modeling: income tax on the estate/trust vs heir differs (Form 1041 compressed brackets vs heir’s ordinary rates) — CPA to run both before/after distribution of A.

Timeline

BEFORE DEATH (now → DOD)
  • Assemble: trust original, pour-over will, certification of trust, deeds, insurance declarations, tax bills, solar NEM agreement, recent assessments, prior 3 years of 1040/540, beneficiary designations, oil/sewer/appliance records.
  • Baseline written appraisal of A and B (dated; keep with trust) — feeds step-up validation and Prop 19 cap math.
  • Attorney & CPA intake meetings; pre-commitment signatures on the IPS/pre-commitment page; route Briefing Sheet for written answers.
  • Insurance audit: A (vacant-home endorsement risk if DOD surprise), B (landlord DP-3 + liability umbrella ≥ $1M), earthquake for both.
  • Recheck law annually (next: Jan 2027 Rev. Proc.; Feb 2027 Prop 19 allowance adjustment; FY2027-28 SF Prop H first bill; CA legislation session Oct 2027).
DAY 0–3 · DOD
Secure A physically; photograph/video contents; shut off/secure water and gas; notify insurer (vacancy clauses 30–60 days!); notify attorney & CPA; notify tenants if occupied; retain certified death certificates (order 10+).
DAY 1–14
Trustee locates original trust; confirms distribution path (in-kind vs sell vs hold); obtains date-of-death appraisal (certified appraiser; both properties; Form 706 backup if triggered). Inventory assets + debts; open trust EIN (Form SS-4); notify banks/brokerages; stop autopay deliberately; value securities at DOD (avg of high/low for estate purposes, or alternate election).
DAY 30–45 · County filings
Record Affidavit of Death of Trustee + certified death certificate with Santa Clara County Recorder; file Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (BOE-502-A) with recording (or as county instructs — penalties attach); file Prop 19 exclusion claim (intergenerational transfer, assessor form — BOE-58/BOE-19 series, county-specific) if live-in path; claim homeowners exemption ($7,000 assessed reduction) when anyone qualifies; if occupation will happen, move within statutory window.
DAY 45–90 · IRS
Determine necessity & compute Form 706 (9-month due date, extendable 6 months via Form 4768 — extension of time to file, not pay); elect alternate valuation only if tax falls; portability DSUE on timely 706 even if $0; Form 709 for any prior-year gifts already filed; check Rev. Proc. 2025-32 numbers against final-year tables.
DAY 90–180
Haircut all estimates against the appraisal; resolve debts; decide distribution mechanics; like-kind decision point (if any) — signed advice before listing; if selling: pre-listing inspection (roof, sewer lateral, WDO, solar warranty transfer), TDS/SPQ disclosures, staging, list; if renting: execute written lease, lead-paint (pre-1978) pamphlet, SF/city rent-control verification, security deposit limits; if living: occupancy + homeowners exemption filed.
DAY 180–270
Trust distributions (in-kind deed vs cash); Form 1041 (fiduciary income tax) if estate/trust gross income > $600; CA Form 541 if gross > $10,000 or net > $1,000; beneficiaries get K-1s; retitling of accounts (new brokerage trust/individual); basis letters to beneficiaries (FMV documentation — heirs need it forever); final 1040 (Form 1040 + Schedule A as applicable; CA final 540).
MONTH 9 · FORM 706 DUE
Hard stop. If alternate valuation elected, values freeze at month 6. Payment due with return (interest from day 0); §6166 unavailable for bare real estate (business test) — liquidity must come from the sale, a loan, or prior planning.
MONTH 12 · true-up
Supplemental property tax bills arrive (prorated to DOD-transfer date — budget, don’t panic); verify reassessment status on A (exclusion granted? base+allowance math on the assessor’s notice); SFB/Prop H first bill FY2027-28+; update insurance to final occupancy; confirm Prop 19 continuing-residence compliance; re-baseline this entire model with real appraisal + real bills.
YEAR 1–3 · settle
Claim form deadline (3 years from transfer) — do not miss; supplemental/derivation notices; if exclusion denied → appeal (assessment appeals board, 60-day window from notice); final trust accounting delivered; close trust or certificate of assets distributed; archive: death certificate, appraisal, 706/541, deeds, bills, this journal.
ANNUAL · forever
Re-run projections with actual bills; Prop 19 occupancy watch; Form 540 line 62 check for any >$1M CA taxable year; extreme-scenario review each January; legislation watch (Sources); journal updates (Journal).

What changes by path (only the deltas)

Path “live in A”

  • Day 30–45: Prop 19 exclusion claim + homeowners exemption
  • Within 1 year: occupancy (locked-in reassessment base)
  • Year 1–∞: keep proof of occupancy (voter reg, DL, utility bills, returns filed at A) — Prop 19 is a continuing condition
  • Years 2–5: §121 clock starts (2-of-5) for future sale

Path “rent A”

  • Day 30–45: reassessment to FMV (~$71,400/yr) — no Prop 19
  • Day 45–90: lease (lead-paint pamphlet, rent control/just-cause verification, security deposit cap), tenant screening, habitability
  • Every year: depreciate building 27.5-yr; track recapture exposure (25%)
  • Sale later: recapture tax + gain tax; 1031 window analysis first

Path “sell A”

  • Day 45–90: seller disclosures; pre-list repairs; list
  • Day 90–180: escrow; county transfer tax $1.10/$1,000 (seller customarily); deed & recorded documents; proceeds to staging account (Treasury auctions via TreasuryDirect)
  • Day 180–270: final costs + net-sheet reconciliation; Form 1041 if in trust
  • Months 1–36: M1 phased equity purchases (Mixed paths); IPS-governed

Documents checklist (both paths, print-friendly)

Legal / trust

  • Original revocable trust + all amendments; certification of trust
  • Will (pour-over) + any codicils; POAs (financial/health)
  • Deeds: A (current), B (current); preliminary title for A
  • Beneficiary designations: IRAs, 401k/403b, life, annuities, TOD/POD
  • Marital/community property characterization memos
  • Prior gift tax returns (709) — unified credit consumption
  • Insurance declarations (A, B, umbrella); claim history CLUE
  • Solar NEM agreement & PG&E interconnection docs (transferability)

Financial / tax

  • Date-of-death appraisal (A) + broker price opinions (B, if needed)
  • Final 1040/540 (decedent); Form 1041/541 for trust/estate
  • Form 706 if gross estate > threshold (or portability filing)
  • Form 502/PCOR; assessor exclusion claim; supplemental bills
  • Cost-basis worksheets (all assets, incl. B’s original 1983-era carryover data if community/separate)
  • Depreciation schedules for B (accumulated) — needed even if only A is sold
  • Estate liquidity statement (cash, proceeds, insurance proceeds)
  • Decision journal exports (journal) — the “why we chose” evidence

Statutory text: IRC §1014, §121, §2001, §2631, §6166; RTC §51, §63.2, §480; CA PCOR BOE-502-A; IRS Form 706/709/1041/4768/SS-4/6252; CA FTB Form 540/541; all citations with links. Deadlines: 9-month 706 · 3-year Prop 19 claim · 45-day-class recording items. When in doubt: file the claim anyway — denial costs nothing but time.
