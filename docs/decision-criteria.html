Decision Framework

Decision Criteria & What Changes the Answer

Agree on decision rules before final figures arrive. This prevents reverse-engineering the "right" answer from one projection.

Pre-commit rule: Write down which path wins under which conditions before updated appraisals, rent comps, tax returns, and legal advice are received. Record the decision, date, owner, and reason in the decision log below.
