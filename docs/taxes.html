Taxes · Estate ≠ Basis ≠ Property tax — never conflate them

The three taxes that decide everything — separated

Ninety percent of inheritance-planning confusion comes from mixing up three independent systems. Here is each one, what triggers it, who pays, which form, and the verified 2026 numbers — all enacted law, tagged as such; proposals are labeled separately.

Bottom line on the three lenses: (1) estate tax is probably $0 — verify gross estate vs ≈ $20,900,000 (2040) with the CPA before concluding; (2) basis is kind — step-up erases the pre-death gain; selling near death costs nothing in income tax; waiting 10–50 years taxes later appreciation at fed 0/15/20%+NIIT + CA ordinary+1% MHSA; (3) property tax is the silent killer — no Prop 19 on rent/second-home/sell ⇒ ≈ $71,400/yr vs ≈ $27,150/yr with a compliant live-in claim. The whole decision rotates on lens 3 and on occupancy intent.
