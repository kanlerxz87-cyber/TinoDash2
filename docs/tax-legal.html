Tax, Legal & Compliance

Federal, California, Santa Clara County, and San Francisco considerations relevant to Property A/B. This is a planning map, not advice. Verify every item against current official sources and qualified counsel.

Separate the three tax layers: (1) Estate/gift/inheritance tax — whether a transfer triggers tax/filing; (2) Income-tax basis — step-up/carryover basis, capital gains, depreciation recapture; (3) Property-tax assessment — Prop 13/19 assessed value, reassessment, exemptions. These are different systems and can point in different directions.
