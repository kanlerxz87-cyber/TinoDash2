Attorney questions

  • Confirm trust terms, distribution timing, trustee powers, and beneficiary occupancy rights.
  • Confirm Prop 19 eligibility, filing deadlines, and value-cap mechanics for this fact pattern.
  • Review Property B leases/rent rules and any SF measure applicability before occupancy/rent changes.
  • Assess 1031 feasibility/risks only if considered; no exchange steps before advice.
  • Assess estate liquidity, portability, prior gifts, and Form 706 strategy.

CPA / tax adviser questions

  • Model same-tax-year sale vs. later sale, including CA ordinary-income treatment of capital gains and 1% tax over $1M taxable income.
  • Document step-up basis and holding period; plan for depreciation/recapture if rented.
  • Model Prop 19 property-tax outcomes for eligible vs. non-eligible scenarios.
  • Price transfer tax, recording fees, selling costs, and tax reserves into net proceeds.
  • Prepare written IPS/tax-aware reinvestment plan before proceeds arrive.
