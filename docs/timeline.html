Deadlines & Decision Timeline

Deadlines are shown as a timeline and table. Confirm all dates with qualified counsel; legal deadlines depend on facts, filing elections, and jurisdictions.

Not legal advice. Dates below are planning flags based on official guidance summaries. Confirm with estate attorney, CPA, and county assessor before relying on any deadline.

Visual timeline

Before transfer (now–2040/2050)
Prepare the decision system. Build private estate inventory; obtain independent valuations/comps; validate Prop 19 eligibility; review leases/local rules for Property B; document investing policy; prepare estate liquidity plan; ask about portability/prior gifts; track law changes annually.
Outcome: no decision made on incomplete evidence.
At transfer / date of death event
Document basis and facts. Obtain written date-of-death valuation for Property A; identify trust terms; secure property, insurance, utilities, and records; inventory assets/liabilities.
Source anchors: IRS Pub 551, IRS Estate Tax.
Within 1 year of transfer
Prop 19 primary-residence action (if applicable). If claiming parent-child/grandchild exclusion, transferee must occupy as primary residence and file homeowner's/disabled veteran's exemption within 1 year. Hard deadline
Source: CA BOE Prop 19.
Within 3 years of transfer (or before transfer to 3rd party)
File Prop 19 exclusion claim. File BOE-19-P/BOE-19-G as applicable. Earlier is better to avoid supplemental assessment issues.
Source: CA BOE Prop 19.
9 months after death (+ extensions as applicable)
Form 706 / portability decision. File federal estate tax return if required; even if not required, consider timely complete Form 706 to elect portability of unused exclusion (DSUE).
Source: IRS Estate Tax, Form 706 instructions.
Same tax year as inheritance (decision point)
Model a same-year sale before deciding. Compare same-year sale vs. hold vs. later sale for basis, capital gain, CA taxable income, and 1% Mental Health Services Tax exposure.
Source: FTB capital gains.
Before any sale
Net proceeds checklist. Price transfer tax, recording fees, broker commissions, legal/escrow, repairs, staging, and tax reserves. Confirm 1031 exchange only with qualified intermediary/tax advice before closing.
Sources: local recorder/treasurer; IRS Pub 551 (basis).
Before proceeds arrive
Investment Policy Statement (IPS). Define target allocation, liquidity reserve, rebalancing, phased entry, drawdown limits, and who can change it.
Planning control; not a market prediction.
Annual review
Re-run model and verify law changes. Update valuations, rents, insurance, tax law, interest rates, and household goals. Track proposed legislation separately from enacted law.
Use official federal/state/local sources.
