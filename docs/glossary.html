Glossary & Methodology

Plain-language definitions and modeling rules used in this dashboard.

Glossary

TermPlain-language meaningWhy it matters here
Step-up in basisFor inherited property, the tax basis is generally reset to fair market value at date of death (with exceptions).Can reduce capital gains tax if Property A is sold near transfer. IRS Pub 551
Prop 13California rule limiting assessed value increases (generally up to 2%/year) until change in ownership/new construction.Explains low assessed values on long-held Property B. BOE Pub 29
Prop 19California rule changing parent-child/grandchild transfers and base-year value transfers. Primary residence occupancy and filing deadlines matter.Can preserve or lose favorable property-tax treatment for Property A. BOE Prop 19
Portability (DSUE)A surviving spouse may be able to use deceased spouse's unused federal estate exclusion if timely elected on Form 706.Relevant to estate planning even when no estate tax is immediately due. IRS Estate Tax
Imputed rentThe economic value of living in a home you own instead of paying market rent.Needed to compare live-in path fairly against rent/sell paths.
Sequence-of-returns riskBad returns early after investing a lump sum can permanently impair outcomes.Key risk for sell-and-reinvest path after inheritance.
Reinvestment riskRisk that cash/Treasury proceeds must be reinvested later at lower yields or worse prices.Key downside of phased Treasury parking strategy.
Cash dragHolding lower-return cash/Treasuries while markets rise, reducing expected return.Tradeoff against crash-entry protection.
Depreciation recaptureTax on depreciation previously claimed when property is sold.Relevant if Property A is rented and depreciation is taken.
1031 exchangeTax-deferral mechanism for qualifying like-kind real estate exchanges; strict rules apply.Possible but not default; requires advance qualified advice.
TRA (Tax Rate Area)Geographic area determining property tax rate components.Needed for accurate Property A property tax projection.

Methodology

  • Privacy-first: No names, addresses, ZIP codes, parcel IDs, or exact traceable public-record values in public outputs. All currency rounded to nearest $10.
  • Ranges over false precision: Where inputs are uncertain, use low/base/high ranges and label them.
  • Official-source verification: Every table row should have an official source link where possible; unverified values are blank/flagged.
  • Separate tax layers: Estate/gift tax, income-tax basis, and property-tax assessment are modeled separately.
  • Scenario, not prediction: Black swan and baseline projections are stress tests, not forecasts.
  • Decision gates first: "What would change the answer" appears before recommendation.
  • Rebuild on change: After input changes, rebuild model/site and check whether ranking moved.

Definitions are simplified for planning. Consult primary sources and professionals.
