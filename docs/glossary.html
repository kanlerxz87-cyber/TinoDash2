Glossary — grouped

Plain-English definitions, grouped the same way the navigation is grouped. External links go to the official explainer (IRS, FTB, BOE, Treasury). Not legal advice; the attorney/CPA always outranks this page.

Estate & gift

TermMeaning
Gross estateEverything the decedent owned (or had incidents of ownership over) at death: real estate at FMV, accounts, business interests, insurance payable to estate, lifetime transfers with retained powers. Basis-adjusted assets count at full FMV.
Basic exclusion amountHow much may transfer free of federal estate/gift tax. $15,000,000 (2026) per person; indexed from 2027; top rate 40% above. IRS
Unified creditThe credit version of the exclusion: tentative tax computed then credited away up to the exclusion; deceased-spousal unused exclusion (DSUE) is portable via Form 706.
Unified credit equivalentThe exclusion expressed as a dollar credit — same number, different ledger page.
Portability / DSUESurviving spouse may use the deceased spouse’s unused exclusion if a timely Form 706 is filed (Rev. Proc. 2017-34 relief for late small estates).
Annual gift exclusionGifts up to $19,000 (2026) per recipient per year are excluded from gift tax entirely, regardless of the lifetime unified credit.
Form 706Federal estate (and generation-skipping transfer) tax return; due 9 months after death; Form 4768 extends time to file 6 months — never extends time to pay.
Form 709Federal gift tax return; due Apr 15 of following year; required when gifts exceed the annual exclusion or gift-splitting is elected.
Form 1041 / CA Form 541Fiduciary income tax returns for the estate/trust: federal > $600 gross income; CA gross > $10,000 or net > $1,000. K-1s flow to beneficiaries.
GST taxGeneration-skipping transfer tax; exemption tracks the basic exclusion ($15,000,000, 2026).
QTIP / HEMS / bypass trustCommon trust machinery: QTIP = qualified terminable interest property for marital deduction; HEMS = health/education/maintenance/support standard limiting trustee discretion; bypass/credit-shelter = uses first spouse’s exclusion without marital transfer.
In-kind distributionThe trust gives the asset itself (the house) to beneficiaries rather than selling and distributing cash — taxes differ; deed must be recorded.
Date-of-death (DOD) valueFMV on the date the person died (or alternate valuation 6 months later if elected); anchor for basis and for estate inclusion.
Form 706 due / alternate valuationElect AV only if it lowers both gross estate and tax; rare for gain assets.
CA estate/inheritance taxNone. Prop 6 (1982) repealed inheritance tax; pick-up tax died with the federal credit (2005). The only death-adjacent taxes in CA: federal estate, property-tax reassessment, income tax on IRD/gains.

Income & capital-gains tax

TermMeaning
Basis (adjusted)For tax, the yardstick for gain: cost + improvements − depreciation ± other adjustments. Inheritance resets basis to DOD FMV (§1014(a)).
Step-up in basisInherited assets get basis = FMV at death; all appreciation before death is never income-taxed. The single most valuable sentence in this entire brief.
§121 primary-residence exclusion$250,000 (single) / $500,000 (MFJ) of gain excluded on sale of principal residence if owned + used 2 of last 5 years; last-used 2 years before sale; prior use checked.
LTCG brackets (federal)0% / 15% / 20%; breakpoints indexed annually (2026 ≈ shown on Taxes page, projected); 20% for top slices.
NIIT (3.8%)IRC §1411 surtax on the lesser of net investment income or MAGI over $200,000 single / $250,000 MFJ; thresholds NOT indexed.
CA ordinary treatment of gainsCalifornia taxes capital gains as ordinary income (no preferential rate); top marginal 12.3% over ≈ $1,000,000 taxable (2024/2025 schedules, indexed).
1% over $1,000,000 (line 62)CA “Mental Health Services Tax” (Prop 63, 2004; 2025 sources rename “Behavioral Health Services Tax”): (CA taxable income − $1,000,000) × 1% — whole dollars, Form 540 line 62, NOT indexed. See Taxes page worked example.
Unrecaptured §1250 gainGain equal to accumulated depreciation on real property, taxed at max 25% federally; CA taxes at ordinary rates; the rent path’s sleeper cost.
Depreciation (residential rental)27.5-year straight-line on building (excluding land); reduces basis & taxable rent; basis reduction is what §1250 recaptures.
Cost segregationEngineering-based acceleration of depreciation on components (carpets, appliances, land improvements) into 5/7/15-yr classes; increases early deductions; §1245 ordinary recapture on sale.
IRD (income in respect of a decedent)Income earned but not received by death (final wages, accrued dividends, IRA distributions) — NOT stepped-up; taxed to recipient; §691 deduction for estate tax attributable.
§1031 like-kind exchangeSwap investment/business real property for like-kind property; gain deferred; 45-day identify / 180-day close; QI required; no personal-residence property unless post-conversion held for investment.
Installment sale (§453)Spread gain recognition over payments received; depreciation recapture recognized in year of sale regardless; interest on deferred tax.
SALT cap interplayOBBBA (2025) raised the state-and-local-tax deduction cap to $40,000 for 2025, rising 1%/yr through 2029, reverting to $10,000 in 2030; phase-down above $500,000 MAGI (floor $10,000). 2026 cap ≈ $40,400 — verify the current figure at filing.

Property tax & Prop 13 / Prop 19

TermMeaning
Ad valorem“According to value” — the tax is on assessed value times rate.
Proposition 13 (1978)Caps general property tax at 1% + limits annual assessment growth to 2%; reassessment only on change in ownership or new construction. BOE
Factored base year valueThe original 1975 (or change-of-ownership) value trended forward ≤2%/yr — Prop B’s $114,250 and A’s $550,540 are exactly this.
Change in ownershipAny transfer of present interest (sale, gift, death distribution) triggers reassessment to FMV unless an exclusion applies. Death itself is a change in ownership.
Prop 19 (2020)Parent/grandchild→child/grandchild exclusion limited to principal residence; child must occupy within 1 year and continuously thereafter; FMV above (factored base + $1,044,586 Feb 2025–Feb 2027) is added to new assessed value. BOE — Prop 19
Prop 19 allowanceThe $1,000,000 (indexed; $1,044,586 in Feb 2025–Feb 2027 window) added to factored base when computing excluded value. Adjusts every 2 years (FHFA CA HPI). “Carry the allowance forward” = re-index it at each window in models and claims.
Homeowner’s exemption$7,000 off assessed value for owner-occupied principal residence — trivial money, file anyway.
TRA (Tax Rate Area)The 2-digit/4-digit code on the bill combining district levies and bond rates; Composite rate = 1% + bonds for that TRA. Santa Clara has 900+; SF has its own map.
Mello-RoosSpecial tax district for infrastructure; appears on the bill; not subject to the 1% cap; not assessed value.
Supplemental assessmentMid-year reassessment bill issued after change in ownership or new construction; prorated from month of transfer.
PCOR (BOE-502-A)Preliminary change of ownership report filed with the county recorder at recording; late filing penalties. The assessor’s first look at the transfer.
Documentary transfer taxState-authorized county tax on recorded transfers for consideration. Santa Clara: $1.10 per $1,000 ($0.55/$500). SF: tiered, $0.50–6.0% full consideration. Not due on bona fide gifts/death via trust (no consideration).
SF Prop H (Nov 2026)“Stronger Muni for All” parcel tax: $129 single-family ≤3,000 sqft; multifamily ≤5,000 sqft $249; >5,000 sqft adds $0.195/sqft; indexed; FY2027-28 → 2042; 65+ exemption; ≤$65/unit/yr rent-controlled pass-through. SFMTA

Investment, staging & Black Swans

TermMeaning
CAGRCompound annual growth rate — the “average” that actually compounds: (end/start)^(1/years)−1.
σ (volatility)Standard deviation of annual returns; S&P 500 σ ≈ 15.6% since 1926; use 16% for modeling, 22% for stress.
Sequence-of-returns riskSame average return, wildly different outcomes depending on order — worst at the start of a withdrawal or staging window.
Staging / phased entryHold proceeds in T-bills; buy equities in equal installments over 24–36 months; bounds worst-case entry regret at cost of ceding straight-line bull runs.
Dollar-cost averagingFixed-dollar periodic purchases; automatic buy-more-low; psychologically and mathematically stabilizes entry.
Drawdown / peak-to-troughMax loss from a prior peak to the subsequent trough: DJIA 1929–32 ≈ −86/89%; S&P 2000–02 ≈ −49%; 2007–09 ≈ −55%; 2020 ≈ −34% (6 weeks).
Black SwanTaleb’s term: outlier with extreme impact, rationalized only in hindsight. 1929, 1973–74, 1987, 2000, 2008, 2020 qualify operationally.
Lognormal / fat tailsEquity returns ≠ normal distribution; left tail is fatter; why σ underestimates crash odds; why ladders beat lump sums for the regret-averse.
Monte Carlo (seeded)Randomize path 4,000×; report percentile bands; our seed (20261125) makes runs reproducible; still illustrative, never predictive.
T-bills vs notes vs bondsBills ≤ 1 yr (discount, no coupon); notes 2–10 yr; bonds 10+ yr. 3M–2Y ladder keeps principal risk ≈ 0 while awaiting equity entry. Treasury daily yield curve
Opportunity costThe $355,500/yr an index might earn vs $0 in staging cash — the reason staging is a hedge, not a plan.
Rebalance bands±5% relative tolerance around targets; sell/buy to band edge; never time to “feel” cheap/expensive.
Expense ratioAnnual fund fee drag; target < 0.10%; 0.03% total-market index is the reference point.

Process & documents

TermMeaning
Trust (revocable living)Grantor keeps full control; assets avoid probate; on death becomes irrevocable; trustee distributes per instrument; no court supervision required.
Successor trusteePerson/institution who steps in at incapacity/death to administer the trust.
Certification of trustShort form trust excerpt (bank-friendly) proving existence and trustee authority without revealing full terms.
Pour-over willCatch-all will sending probate assets into the trust; backstop, not the plan.
Affidavit of death of trusteeRecorded instrument transferring trust property on trustee’s death; pairs with deed for A.
PCOR (BOE-502-A)Preliminary change of ownership report; see Property tax group.
Date-of-death appraisalCertified appraisal as of DOD; anchors basis; satisfies IRS Pub 551 documentation; 3 copies + digital.
Alternate valuation§2032 election: value 6 months post-death if both value and tax decrease — never for low-gain assets.
Step-transaction doctrineIRS may collapse formally separate steps into one for tax; e.g., pre-arranged sale right after distribution.
K-1Beneficiary’s share of trust/estate income & deductions; feeds heir’s 1040.
CLUE reportInsurance claim history (C.L.U.E.); underwriters pull it; affects premium and eligibility.
NEM (net energy metering) agreementPG&E solar interconnection terms; must transfer on sale; affects value; get NEM version in writing.
