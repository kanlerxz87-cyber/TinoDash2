Mixed paths — and the Black-Swan staged-investing path

The three pure choices (live / rent / sell) are rarely optimal as stated. This page models seven mixed paths, with the requested Black-Swan variant — park the sale proceeds in 3–24-month Treasuries for 24–36 months, then phase into stocks — front and center. All have taxes, debt, occupancy, capital cost and liquidity marked.

Staging is entry-risk averaging, not a return promise. If stocks rip upward during the staging window (2016–2021), staged entry underperforms lump sum by the T-bill-vs-equity spread. Staging is chosen for robustness — i.e., to minimize the regret of buying the 2040 top — not to beat the market.

The headline mixed path — M1: Sell A → Treasuries ladder → phase into equities (24–36 months)

Mechanics (to be written into the IPS before death — see Investment Policy)

  1. Within 30 days of closing the A sale: proceeds land in a T-bill/Treasury-only money-market or laddered 3M/6M/12M bills bought at auction (TreasuryDirect) — state-tax-free interest, near-zero principal risk.
  2. Months 1–36: buy an equal slice of a broad US equity index (e.g., total-market ETF, expense ≈ 0.03–0.10%) on the 1st of each month — 24 or 36 equal installments.
  3. Phase-in ends: 100% equities (or policy target), hold buy-and-hold; dividends reinvested; rebalance annually ±5% bands.
  4. Crash override: if the index falls ≥ 20% from staging start, accelerate remaining installments 2× (rules-based, pre-committed — never ad-hoc).
  5. Report to household at months 6/12/24/36; annual re-verification of this whole model (see Decision Journal).

Choosing among mixed paths — the ranking logic

Wealth-maximization order

  1. M1/M5 (sell + staged reinvest) — highest expected compounding, high liquidity, lowest regret in drawdowns
  2. M2 (live 2–5y then sell) — adds §121 shelter; equal to M1 if lifestyle benefit ≥ transaction costs of two moves
  3. M4 (live A + rent B’s 3rd unit) — a financing overlay on M2, not a standalone path; adds ≈ $36,000–$54,000/yr
  4. M6 (1031) — deferral swap; only with adviser + committed CA real-estate strategy
  5. M3 (rent then sell) / M7 (second home) — lifestyle-only or forced-wait scenarios

What breaks the ranking

  • Genuine occupancy commitment → M2/M4 outrank M1.
  • Appraisal below ≈ $4,700,000 or rent comparables ≥ $12,000/mo → all paths re-run; rent path improves but rarely wins.
  • Form 706 exposure > $0 → M5 liquidity ladder is mandatory, not optional.
  • Crash at reinvestment date → M1 gains vs lump sum; ordering unchanged.
  • Trust forbids sale without co-heir consent → revisit with attorney (see Q13).
