Black-Swan Stress Lab — what can actually happen to the reinvested millions

Growth and inflation are never consistent over a 50-year horizon (1926–2025: S&P 500 ≈ +10.3%/yr nominal, ≈ +7.0% real; 1970s: +1.6%/yr real for the decade; 2000s: negative). This lab stress-tests the sell path against history’s worst tapes, prices the insurance value of the staged entry, and fixes an annual review cadence.

Staging is a hedge whose premium (≈ 5–7 pp of forgone equity upside in a straight-line bull tape) is paid every time. It is justified by expected regret minimization across a 50-year holding period, not by return enhancement. If the household’s risk tolerance is genuinely high, M1 may collapse to “X months only.” Decide in writing, see IPS.

Structural “impossible-looking” risks worth one line each

  • Confiscatory wealth tax / federal estate exclusion cut — OBBBA indexes post-2026; Congress can amend anytime. Mitigation: annual re-check + flexible gifting strategy ($19,000/yr annual exclusion 2026; Crummey trusts) if law turns adverse.
  • CA re-creates a state death tax — none exists today; tracked as proposed only (see Sources → legislation watch). No current bill qualifies.
  • Prop 13 reform (split roll) — periodic legislature interest (SCA-type measures); would raise reassessment risk on long-held A. Watch items on Sources.
  • Seismic event, Property A — CA earthquake insurance is a separate policy (HO excludes earth movement); retrofit quotes on Property A.
  • Trust litigation / co-heir dispute — pre-commitment signatures (policy page) are the mitigation.
  • Solar NEM agreement value — ensure transferability on change of ownership; PG&E interconnection paperwork in trust records.
  • Rate shock — 10Y ≈ 4.9–5.1% (Nov 2026): T-bill ladder income ≈ $220,000–$260,000/yr while staged; recorded here so nobody forgets the upside of the hedge.

Annual Extreme-Scenario Review (to be calendared every January)

  1. Re-pull official exclusion (Rev. Proc. each October), CPI, T-bill/2Y/10Y, S&P 500 YTD, SF/Santa Clara rate books, Prop 19 allowance window (Feb odd years).
  2. Re-run this page’s stress table with current net-proceeds number; log deltas in Decision Journal.
  3. Confirm staging rules still signed; confirm no accidental discretionary overrides happened.
  4. Confirm insurance (A, B) adequate; re-quote if premium moved > 25%.
  5. File annual reminders: Form 706 deadline (9 months), Prop 19 claim (3 years), SF Prop H exemption application (if 65+), Form 540 line 62 check for any >$1M CA taxable year.
