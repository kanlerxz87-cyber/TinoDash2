Housekeeping rules

  • Any figure changed anywhere on this site ⇒ same-day journal entry with before/after values and source link.
  • “Needs review” items from Flagged for Review get closed with the closing date and the official link that closes them.
  • Superseded entries are struck through, never deleted — the audit trail is the product.
  • Review cadence: January every year, 60 minutes, both household members, coffee mandatory.
