How to discover missing information (practical)

Official/public sources

  • CA BOE Prop 19/Prop 13 publications and county assessor forms.
  • IRS estate tax, Form 706 instructions, Pub 551 for basis.
  • FTB capital gains and Form 540 instructions/booklet for CA tax treatment.
  • County recorder/treasurer/assessor for TRA, transfer tax, recording fees.
  • SF Rent Board / official city ordinances for Property B rules.

Private/professional sources

  • Qualified appraisal (date-of-death FMV).
  • CPA tax projection (same-year sale, rent, depreciation, recapture, CA $1M surtax exposure).
  • Estate attorney (trust terms, Prop 19, portability, liquidity).
  • Insurance broker quotes (owner, landlord, umbrella, hazard).
  • Property inspections and contractor quotes.
  • Independent rental comps / property manager opinion.

Planning aid only. Resolve blockers before irreversible decisions.
