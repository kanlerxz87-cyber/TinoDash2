Questions & missing information — before any decision is reasonable

This is the complete register. Every item names who answers it, how to get the answer, and what it changes. Until the Flagged ones close, “sell” is a strong leaning but not a verdict. Sorted by group; ★ = blocking for the baseline recommendation.

Status key: Verified = confirmed against official source · Estimate = modeled · Flagged = cannot close from public official sources · Missing = not yet in hand.  Items marked ★ move the headline ranking if answered adversely.

A · Estate, trust & identity of the gift

  1. ★ What assets constitute the inheritance, and in what form? (A + cash amount; any IRAs/brokerage/life insurance with TOD/POD; business interests) — Discover: inventory with attorney; ask decedent now if possible; beneficiary statements. Missing
  2. ★ Trust instrument type and distribution clauses (revocable vs AB/QTIP; HEMS; trustee sale authority; spendthrift; disinheritance/contest clauses; co-trustee duties) — Discover: certified copy of full trust from settlor; attorney review letter. Missing
  3. ★ Debts, liens, taxes, or equalization obligations attached to A (mortgage, HELOC, property-tax arrears, Mello-Roos, special assessments, trust debts, funeral) — Discover: preliminary title report; county lien search; last 3 tax bills; credit report of estate after DOD. Missing
  4. ★ Co-heirs / beneficiaries and their intent: does anyone else inherit A or residue? Do they consent to sale? Buy-sell formula? — Discover: attorney letter to all beneficiaries; occupancy questionnaire. Missing
  5. Date of death (actual or projected window): 2026–2030 vs 2040–2050 materially changes exclusion indexing, Prop 19 allowance, and CA law exposure windows — Discover: actuarial estimate vs family health picture (private). 2040–2050 assumed
  6. Is Property A the grantor’s principal residence at death? (Prop 19 condition #1) — Discover: utility/driver-license/voter records; attorney affidavit. Unverified
  7. Married or single at death; community/joint/separate character of A (double step-up §1014(b)(6)) — Discover: title, trust recitals, CPA characterization memo. Missing
  8. Prior taxable gifts by grantor (lifetime Form 709 filings) — consume unified credit before the $15M even applies — Discover: ask now; IRS transcripts (Form 4506-T) at death. Missing
  9. Mortgage or reverse mortgage on A? A reverse mortgage (HECM) is due in full at death — appraisal needed to decide payoff vs sale — Discover: lender statement; HUD search. Missing
  10. Fractional ownership history (tenants in common with siblings?) — affects §121, Prop 19 eligibility, valuation discounts — Discover: deed chain; title report. Missing

B · Tax computation inputs

  1. ★ Combined gross estate value vs projected exclusion (≈ $20.9M 2040 / ≈ $26.5M 2050): above threshold ⇒ Form 706; 40% on excess — Discover: household net-worth statement; CPA projection with 2.4% indexing (or final Rev. Proc.). Projected
  2. Household filing status & income profile (single / MFJ; ordinary wages; other gains; NIIT MAGI vs $200k/$250k) — Discover: CPA intake; last 3 × (1040 + CA 540). Missing
  3. Portability (DSUE) election economics: surviving spouse? If so, is a $0 706 filing worth preserving DSUE (Rev. Proc. 2017-34 late-election relief, 5 years)? — Discover: attorney + CPA joint memo. Missing
  4. ★ Form 540 line 62 exposure in any sale year: (CA taxable − $1,000,000) × 1%; threshold not indexed; whole dollars — model the actual year of sale (same-year-as-inheritance vs later) — Discover: CPA stacking computation using real brackets & deductions. Rule verified; inputs missing
  5. Depreciation schedule for B (and any A rental years) — accumulated depreciation drives 25% unrecaptured §1250 recapture; CA conforms at ordinary rates — Discover: prior-year 4562s; reconstruct if missing (Form 3115 change of accounting). Missing
  6. §121 two-of-five-years test feasibility for the live-then-sell path: use counts from DOD distribution or from move-in? Ownership counts from death (§ 121(d)(3)(B) acquisition from decedent: use from date of death?) — Discover: CPA technical memorandum; do not self-assess. Law clear; application unverified
  7. §1031 appetite (replace A with other CA rental): holding period before conversion counts; QI engagement before signing; boot; related-party — Discover: CPA + attorney joint session; written 1031 memo if pursued. Missing — do not sign anything first
  8. State nexus on the invested proceeds: any CA exit planned (residency change)? Community property double step-up if applicable? — Discover: residency questionnaire; CA FTB residency audit triggers awareness. Missing
  9. Annual gifting capacity to shrink estate below exclusion before 2040 ($19,000 × recipients × years; Crummey trusts; direct tuition/medical unlimited) — Discover: estate-freeze illustration; attorney drafting if elected. Missing
  10. Alternate valuation election: only if both value and tax fall — almost never for a gain-asset — Discover: CPA runs both 706 variants at death. Determinable only at DOD

C · Properties — condition, value, market

  1. ★ Independent, written date-of-death (or now) appraisal of A — county-licensed appraiser; interior inspection; solar included in contributory value? — Discover: two appraisals (list-side and estate-side); keep both. Missing
  2. ★ Three signed comparable leases for A (not Zestimate): is $10,000/mo supportable? What does the micro-market actually pay for 1,800–2,600 sqft SFH near Apple? — Discover: 2 local broker price opinions + 3 executed lease comps; interview 2 property managers (fee quote + vacancy quote). Letting paths = floor until done
  3. Property A condition & capital needs: roof age, sewer lateral, WDO/termite, foundation/cripple wall, seismic retrofit, cast-iron vs PEX, HVAC/water heater, solar array age & warranty, NEM tariff version (NEM 1/2/3) — Discover: pre-purchase-style inspections now (second opinion later at DOD). Missing
  4. Property B condition: soft-story status below SF mandatory threshold? Private sewer lateral compliance certificate (SFB: transfers on sale)? Lead paint (pre-1978 triplex → RRP rules)? Mold? — Discover: SF DBI records request; lateral inspection; WDO. Missing
  5. Assessed-value history integrity: verify 2026–27 bills both counties; locate TRA of A (flagged #3); locate B’s special assessments causing ≈2% implied rate (flagged #1) — Discover: call Santa Clara Assessor & SF Assessor; pull rate books: scc-assessor.org · sfassessor.org. Flagged
  6. ★ Insurance quotes, not averages: A owner-occupied HO-3 ($200k dwelling endorsement? full replacement cost endorsement?), B DP-3 landlord + liability umbrella $1–2M; earthquake endorsements both; carriers' CA availability — Discover: broker quotes in writing; if admitted market fails → FAIR Plan + DIC wrap (cost modeled on Property A). Averages only today
  7. Market direction at 2040: Apple campus cycle, Cupertino school ratings, Prop 13-adjacent bond levies (school bonds in TRA), Bay Area migration — Discover: annual re-check (already scheduled in annual review). Scenario-based
  8. B’s tenancy quality & rent-control coverage (Costa-Hawkins; vacancy decontrol; allowable annual increase; just-cause eviction; lead-paint registration; DBI rental registration) — Discover: SF Rent Board counseling appointment; lease file audit. Missing
  9. Rent on B could rise if 3rd unit filled: market ≈ $3,000–$4,500/mo; affects live-vs-sell math by ≈ $36,000–$54,000/yr — Discover: same lease-comp exercise as #22. Missing
  10. Utility actuals: A solar true-up statements 12 months (NEM credit/liability); B water $330–$400 (submeter?), electric $30–$70 — Discover: 12 months of PG&E + SFPUC statements. Missing

D · Household & policy

  1. ★ Occupancy intent, signed and dated: will any heir occupy A as principal residence (≤1-year Prop 19 condition; ≥24-month commitment for sanity)? If undecided → both paths costed identically on Comparison — choose by which regret you prefer. Missing
  2. ★ Risk tolerance & liquidity needs 2040–2060: retirement horizon; tuition; family support; mortgage desires; business ventures — Discover: household finance worksheet; IPS risk questionnaire. Missing
  3. Non-financial salience of A (family home? caregiving? sentimental?) — quantify in dollars as imputed-rent offset: “we’d pay $X/mo to control A” — Discover: structured household discussion; document in journal. Missing
  4. Estate-freeze appetite (SLATs, IDGTs, GRATs, family LLC) if estate > exclusion — Discover: attorney advanced-planning memo; only if #11 says so. Missing
  5. Charitable intent (CRTs, DAF bundling) — reduces taxable estate & capital gain — Discover: philanthropy questionnaire. Missing
  6. Insurance/estate mismatch: ILIT-funded policy sized to potential 706 bill (if estate > exclusion) — Discover: illustration from broker; attorney drafts ILIT. Missing

E · Process & administration

  1. Sale same tax year as inheritance vs later — carrying cost ≈ $71,400/yr vs delayed liquidity + appreciation capture; distribution deduction timing (K-1 vs 1041) — Discover: CPA runs year-of vs +1/+5-year variants; table to be attached to Three Taxes. Modeled; needs real numbers
  2. Escrow/title choreography on trust-owned A: affidavit of death of trustee, trust certification at closing, county transfer tax, supplemental bills — sequence in Process — Discover: escrow officer pre-brief; title run before listing. Process known
  3. Records retention: death certificate, 706/709, appraisal, deeds, basis letters — keep forever (basis) and 3–7 years (returns) — Discover: CPA retention letter. Practice
