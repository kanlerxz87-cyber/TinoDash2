Method and exclusions

Property values are current input × (1 + chosen annual appreciation rate)years. For A, before the inheritance year, only market-value scenarios are shown. At inheritance, the non-Prop-19 case models a market-value reset; the Prop 19 case (only if checked) models current base-year value grown by the entered assessment-growth rate plus entered allowance, capped at market value. After transfer, modeled assessment grows by that rate but is capped at projected market value (a simplified Prop 8 sensitivity). B uses its current factored base value with the entered rate and market-value cap. Estimated tax is assessed value × entered actual TRA rate + direct-charge input escalated by cost inflation.

Annual costs use current user-entered water, net electricity, insurance, regular maintenance and capital reserve, escalated by one cost-inflation assumption. B rent uses a single annual growth and vacancy assumption. Rental cash flow subtracts operating costs, property tax and debt service; it excludes income tax, management, legal costs, licensing, special tax treatments, major unplanned repairs and owner labour. Imputed rent is shown separately and is not cash or presumed taxable rent. Update Prop 19 allowance, law, actual bills and rates in transfer year.
