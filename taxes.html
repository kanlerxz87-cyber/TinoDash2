Three different taxes

Estate tax, income-tax basis, and property-tax assessment are unrelated systems that the same events trigger. Mixing them is the classic planning error.

04 · TAXES & LAW, KEPT SEPARATE

Estate tax ≠ income-tax basis ≠ property-tax assessment
