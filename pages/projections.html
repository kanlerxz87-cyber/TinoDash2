4 · Reinvestment risk, stated beside the staging headline

Every staged path on this site shows the cost of its insurance: uninvested tranches earn the Treasury yield, which resets at each maturity. If yields fall (2020-style), staged cash earns almost nothing while markets rise; if yields stay high (2023–25-style), staging looks cheap but equity legs still lag a rising market. The Nov 2026 curve (≈4.3% 3M, ≈4.6% 2Y) is not a forecast of 2040–2050. Official yield source · how staging behaved in 1929/1973/2008/2013/2022.
