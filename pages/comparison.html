1 · First: model a sale in the same tax year as the inheritance

The date-of-death step-up (IRC 1014) resets basis to fair market value at death, so a same-year sale realizes ≈ $0 capital gain. This is the cleanest benchmark in the whole analysis — everything else must beat it.

4 · Method and what the model excludes

Arithmetic (illustrative, pre-tax, rounded to $10)

Property values compound at the case's appreciation range from the 2026 estimate; assessed values follow Proposition 13's 2% cap until the transfer changes ownership (BOE property-tax overview); property tax uses the effective rate implied by household bill history (A ≈1.33%, B ≈2.13%) — the actual tax rate area and direct assessments are unverified. Cash flows (rent, imputed rent, costs) are escalated at the case inflation rate and compounded at the case investment return as if reinvested. The phase-in plans invest equal tranches at months 0/12/24(/36), each earning the Treasury yield until its final tranche deploys. The optional crash stress applies once to invested balances in the chosen year.

Excluded (get adviser figures before deciding): federal and state income tax on invested returns and rents; estate tax; parcel reassessment results under Prop 19; depreciation/recapture on each specific schedule; debt and financing; rent-control limits and lease terms; insurance renewal changes; transaction fees beyond the stack; behaviour and spending policy. The CA 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax sensitivity is on the tax page and does not alter proceeds here.
