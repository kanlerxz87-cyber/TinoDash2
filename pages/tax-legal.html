THREE SEPARATE SYSTEMS · NEVER CONFLATE THEM

Tax & legal map

Separate three ledgers: (1) estate tax — a federal transfer-tax question on the whole estate; (2) income-tax basis — what a future sale gains or loses, set by date-of-death value; (3) property-tax assessment — what the county bills annually under Prop 13/Prop 19. Different numbers, different deadlines, different advisers.

Tax-aware sensitivities checklist

  • Same-year sale vs +1-year sale vs +5-year sale — recompute gain each way. Stack
  • Prop 19 claimed (occupancy) vs not claimed (reassessment to market). Terms
  • §121 on/off for live-then-sell (only with genuine 2-of-5-year occupancy). Ledger 2
  • Depreciation started vs not (rent paths) and recapture at each exit year. Recapture
  • Estate exclusion scenarios single/married × current-law vs lower future exclusion. Ledger 1
  • CA 1% surtax triggered vs not, depending on other income in the sale year. Ledger 2
