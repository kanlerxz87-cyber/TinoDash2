Black Swan Stress Tests & Phased-Investing Path

Testing 50-year non-linear economic regimes and how parking sale proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months softens 1929/2008 crash entry risk.

Phased-Investing Strategy (24–36 Months)

3M–2Y U.S. Treasuries → Gradual Equity DCA

Instead of investing 100% of Property A sale proceeds on Day 1, park proceeds in a 3-month to 2-year Treasury ladder and phase into equities over 24–36 months. Reduces max drawdown during 1929/2008-style entry crashes.

Reinvestment Risk (Beside Headline)

What You Give Up by Staging

1. Rate-Cut Risk: 3M–2Y Treasury yields can drop sharply if the Fed cuts rates during a recession.
2. Opportunity Cost: In a strong bull market (e.g., 1995–1999 or 2023–2025), staging in Treasuries underperforms lump-sum equity investing by ~3%–5% per year.
