Trust Inheritance Process & Deadlines Timeline

Step-by-step execution roadmap from Date of Death (2040–2050) through tax elections, Prop 19 filings, and disposition.

Critical Statutory Deadlines

Day 0 — Date of Death (Transfer Event)
Trust becomes irrevocable. Obtain certified death certificates and secure Property A insurance (switch to estate/landlord policy if vacant).
Within 30–60 Days — Date-of-Death Valuation
Obtain a written, independent appraisal of Property A at Date of Death to establish the stepped-up income-tax basis (IRC § 1014).
Within 150 Days — Change in Ownership Statement
File Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (PCOR) / Change in Ownership Statement with Santa Clara County Assessor.
Within 9 Months — IRS Form 706 (Estate Tax & Portability)
File Form 706 if gross estate exceeds federal exclusion, OR to elect portability of deceased spouse's unused exclusion (DSUE).
Within 1 Year — Prop 19 Occupancy & BOE-19-P
If keeping Property A as primary residence, heir MUST move in and file Homeowners' Exemption + Form BOE-19-P within 1 year of transfer.
Same Tax Year — Model Immediate Sale
Before deciding anything else, model a sale in the same tax year as inheritance (near-zero capital gain due to stepped-up basis).
