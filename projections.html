50-year projections (ranges, not points)

Base case: home growth 3%/yr, inflation 2.5%, factored base +2%/yr (Prop 13 cap — Official). Low/high: 1%/5% growth. All dollars rounded to $10. Change assumptions on the Dashboard — this page rebuilds from the same model.

How projections handle inconsistency

  • Growth and inflation are never constant over 50 years — Black Swan page stress-tests stagflation, crashes, and housing drawdowns. Baseline is a planning anchor, not a promise.
  • Property tax is not projected as % of market (Prop 13 decouples them). It is projected from the factored base.
  • Insurance, water, maintenance inflate at CPI (2.5% base). Solar credit on A is modeled as a small negative utility cost.
  • Rent growth is capped at min(5%+CPI, 10%) per CA Tenant Protection Act (Official — CA AG), and SF Rent Ordinance may be lower. Model uses home-growth rate capped at 5%.
