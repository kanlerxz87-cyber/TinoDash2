TermPlain-English MeaningWhere Used
Ad valoremTax based on value (e.g., 1.185% of assessed value).Property tax
Assessed value / FBYVCounty’s taxable value (Factored Base Year Value). Grows max 2%/yr under Prop 13.Property A/B tables
BOE / LTACA Board of Equalization / Letter to Assessors (official CCPI factors).Historical tables
BOE-19-PForm to claim Prop 19 parent-child exclusion. Must file within 1 year + occupy.Occupy paths
CCPICA Consumer Price Index — BOE inflation factor (capped at 2%).Assessed growth
DSUE PortabilityDeceased Spouse’s Unused Exclusion — married couples can double $15M to $30M via Form 706.Estate tax
FBYVFactored Base Year Value — Prop 13 starting value, indexed 2%/yr.Prop 19 ceiling
FMVFair Market Value — what a willing buyer would pay.Sale / basis
IRC §1014Heir’s basis = FMV at death (stepped-up). Same-year sale = $0 gain.Same-year sale
IRC §1031Like-kind exchange — defer gain by buying replacement property via QI (45/180 days).Rent-then-1031
IRC §121Primary residence exclusion: $250k single / $500k MFJ if lived there 2 of 5 yrs.Live-then-sell
IRC §1250Depreciation recapture — 25% federal tax on depreciation taken.Rent paths
IRC §2001 / §2010Estate tax code: 40% rate; $15M exclusion (OBBBA 2026).Estate tax
MHSA / BHST 1%CA 1% tax on income > $1M (Mental Health Services Tax). Top CA rate 13.3%.Form 540
NEMNet Energy Metering — solar credit when you export more than you use.Property A utilities
OBBBAOne Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21) — made $15M exclusion permanent.Estate tax
Prop 131978 CA law capping assessed growth at 2%/yr; base-year value.All CA property tax
Prop 19 (R&TC §63.2)Parent-child exclusion: heir can keep low basis if they occupy as primary residence.Occupy paths
Prop H (SF)2006: relocation payments for no-fault evictions. 2026 proposed: Muni parcel tax.Property B
SAFMR / FMRHUD Small Area Fair Market Rent / Fair Market Rent — voucher benchmarks.Rent comps
SCC TRASanta Clara County Tax Rate Area — published ad valorem rate (~1.185%).Property A tax
Step-up in basisHeir’s basis reset to FMV at death, erasing prior gain.IRC §1014
Form 540 / 706 / 3840CA income tax / Federal estate tax / CA 1031 tracking forms.Tax filings
IPSInvestment Policy Statement — written rules for investing proceeds, signed before money arrives.Governance
Imputed rentValue of living in your own home (rent you don’t pay). Input for live paths ($6,200/mo).Occupy paths
Reinvestment riskRisk that you invest at a market peak and suffer a crash right after.Headline vs Path 1B
