A little less jargon.
A lot more clarity.

Terms that sound similar can have very different consequences. Ask your advisers how they apply to your circumstances.

TermPlain-language meaningSource
Factored base-year valueThe property-tax base after applicable annual inflation adjustments; not the same as market value.Official source ↗
Income-tax basisThe starting value used to calculate taxable gain or loss; adjustments can apply.Official source ↗
Estate exclusionThe basic federal amount used in calculating the estate and gift tax credit, not a per-beneficiary inheritance allowance.Official source ↗
Portability / DSUEAn election can transfer a deceased spouse’s unused exclusion to a surviving spouse, subject to requirements.Official source ↗
Imputed rentThe hypothetical housing value of living in an owned home; not cash income. Avoid counting it twice.Official source ↗
DepreciationA tax deduction for eligible property over time; it generally reduces adjusted basis.Official source ↗
Reinvestment riskThe risk that maturing securities must be reinvested at less favorable rates.Official source ↗
Like-kind exchangeA qualifying exchange of investment or business real property may defer gain; personal-use property is not automatically eligible.Official source ↗
