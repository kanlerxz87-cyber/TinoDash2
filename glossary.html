Glossary

TermMeaning (plain language)
Factored base year valueProp 13 taxable value: base year value grown ≤2%/yr. Decoupled from market value.
Prop 19 exclusionParent-child transfer without full reassessment — only if heir occupies as primary residence within 1 year, capped at factored base + $1,044,586 (2025–27).
Stepped-up basisHeir's tax basis reset to market value at date of death (IRC §1014). Pre-death gain is never taxed.
§121 exclusion$250k/$500k gain exclusion on primary-residence sale if owned + lived 2 of 5 years.
Unrecaptured §1250Depreciation recapture on rental sale, taxed up to 25% (federal). CA taxes it as ordinary income.
MHST / BHSTCA 1% tax on taxable income above $1M (Form 540 line 62). Only on the excess.
Imputed rentMarket rent avoided by living in your own home. Counted as benefit in live path; otherwise live path looks artificially poor.
Reinvestment riskRisk that phased/Treasury returns lag stock returns in bull markets. Shown beside every staging headline.
Phased investingSell proceeds parked in 3M–2Y Treasuries 24–36 mo, then tranched into stocks. Softens crash-entry risk.
TRATax Rate Area — parcel-level levy area. Determines exact effective rate. Requires APN lookup.
PCOR (BOE-502-A)Preliminary Change of Ownership Report filed with deed at recording.
DSUE / portabilityDeceased spouse's unused estate exemption passed to survivor (requires timely Form 706).
