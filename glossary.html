Plain-language definitions
Reference

A short glossary for the decision table.

Terms below are simplified and not substitutes for statutory definitions. Follow the official links for tax/legal nuance.

Adjusted taxable gifts
Prior taxable gifts generally included in federal estate-tax computations under applicable rules. The actual history must be reconstructed from records. IRS estate-tax FAQ ↗
Basis / stepped-up basis
Tax basis used to determine gain or loss. Inherited basis is generally the date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions. “Step-up” is shorthand; basis may rise or fall and ownership/trust rules matter. IRS Pub. 551 ↗
Capital gain
Amount realized on disposition minus adjusted basis and applicable adjustments. California generally taxes capital gains as ordinary income rather than a separate preferential long-term rate. FTB Schedule CA ↗
Change in ownership / reassessment
A transfer event that may require a county assessor to establish a new base-year value, unless an exclusion applies. County property-tax assessed value is distinct from income-tax basis. BOE assessment guide ↗
Deceased spousal unused exclusion (DSUE) / portability
Potential transfer of unused federal estate-tax exclusion from a deceased spouse to a surviving spouse, generally elected on a timely and complete Form 706. IRS estate-tax FAQ ↗
Depreciation recapture / unrecaptured gain
Tax treatment that can apply when depreciable rental/business property is sold; depreciation allowed or allowable may adjust basis and gain treatment. Exact computation depends on facts and tax rules. IRS Pub. 527 ↗
Estate tax vs inheritance tax
Estate tax is generally imposed on a decedent’s taxable estate; an inheritance tax, where imposed, is generally based on a recipient’s transfer. Do not infer state-level treatment from a federal exclusion. Confirm rules in the jurisdiction and year involved. IRS estate-tax overview ↗
Factored base-year value (FBYV)
California property-tax base-year value adjusted annually by an inflation factor, generally capped at 2% absent specified events; not the property’s market value. BOE property-tax guide ↗
Fair market value (FMV)
Value determined under a relevant legal or appraisal standard for the transaction/date. A broker estimate, assessed value and tax basis are not interchangeable. IRS Pub. 561: valuing property ↗
Imputed rent
Economic value of housing services received by living in a home rather than renting it out or living elsewhere. It is an opportunity-cost concept in this comparison, not necessarily taxable rental income. SEC Investor.gov risk/return overview ↗
Net operating income (NOI)
Rental revenue less operating expenses, typically before financing costs and income tax. Define inclusions consistently; it is not the same as cash available after debt service, capital spending or taxes. IRS Pub. 527 rental income and expenses ↗
Prop 19 intergenerational exclusion
California property-tax change-in-ownership exclusion for eligible family-home/farm transfers under statutory conditions, including qualifying relationships, continued principal-residence use, value rules and filing. BOE Prop 19 ↗
Reinvestment risk
Risk that cash or a maturing short-term fixed-income security must be reinvested at a lower yield. A Treasury ladder can reduce some timing exposure but does not guarantee a future yield. TreasuryDirect bill details ↗
Sequence-of-returns risk
Risk that the order of investment gains/losses affects portfolio value, especially when withdrawals occur. Same average return can produce different outcomes depending on timing. SEC Investor.gov investing basics ↗
Tax Rate Area (TRA)
County identifier that groups the tax-rate components applicable to a parcel. The parcel’s current published rate and special charges must be checked on the official bill/rate book; no TRA is disclosed here. Santa Clara County Assessor ↗
Taxable estate / basic exclusion
Federal estate tax is computed using gross estate, allowable deductions, adjusted taxable gifts, applicable exclusion and other rules. A 2026 exclusion figure is not a lifetime “inheritance allowance” or a future-year guarantee. IRS 2026 provision ↗
§1031 like-kind exchange
Potential deferral of gain for qualifying business/investment real property exchanged for like-kind property under strict rules; not a blanket tax-free sale or a path for property solely used as a personal residence. IRS Form 8824 ↗
