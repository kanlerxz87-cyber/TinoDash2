08 · TERMS THAT MUST NOT BE MIXED UP

Glossary & methodology

Definitions are decision aids; the governing statutes, regulations, and tax forms control. Grouped by the page where each term does the most work.

Property tax & Prop 19 terms

Assessed value / factored base year value
California's property-tax value, capped at +2%/yr by Prop 13 until a reassessment event. Not the market value. SCC Assessor
Prop 19 intergenerational exclusion
Family-home reassessment cap for eligible transfers: new base = old base, or FMV − allowance when FMV exceeds old base + allowance. Requires eligibility, occupancy, and filings. BOE
Tax Rate Area (TRA)
Geographic unit sharing one property-tax rate composition. The parcel's own TRA total is published in the county's annual Compilation of Tax Rates. SCC booklet
Supplemental assessment
A partial-year bill reflecting the value change from a transfer/sale, separate from the regular roll. BOE rules
Documentary transfer tax
Local tax collected at recording: SCC $0.55/$500 (no Property A city tax); SF brackets 0.5–6% applied to the entire value. SCC · SF
Parcel tax (e.g., SF Prop H)
Flat per-parcel amount (not ad valorem), voter-approved; Property B pays the multifamily rate from FY2027–28. SFMTA terms

Estate & income-tax terms

Estate tax
Federal tax on the decedent's gross estate above the basic exclusion ($15,000,000 in 2026, PL 119-21), top rate 40%. Not an automatic tax on every inheritance. IRS
Income-tax basis / step-up
Inherited basis is generally FMV at date of death — a same-year sale realizes roughly zero gain. IRS FAQ
IRC 121 exclusion
$250,000 ($500,000 MFJ) principal-residence gain exclusion; 2-of-5-year ownership+use; once per 2 years; depreciation never excluded. Pub 523
Depreciation / unrecaptured §1250
Rental depreciation reduces basis; on sale it is taxed at a max 25% federal rate, separate from LTCG. Pub 544
NIIT
3.8% net investment income tax on investment income above statutory thresholds. IRS
Form 540 line 62 (Behavioral Health Services Tax)
1% of California taxable income above $1,000,000, whole dollars, never indexed. California taxes capital gains as ordinary income. FTB
Portability / DSUE
A surviving spouse can use the deceased spouse's unused exclusion — only via a timely Form 706 election (9 months + 6; Rev. Proc. 2022-32 late path). Form 706
§1031 like-kind exchange
Deferral for business/investment real property repositions; identification in 45 days, closing in 180. Personal residences don't qualify. IRS

Investing & risk terms

Imputed rent
The value of housing services from living in an owned home — an economic comparison input, never received as cash. Where used
Reinvestment risk
Maturing short-term securities may roll at lower yields (3M T-bills fell from ≈5% to ≈0 during 2008). The phased path's parking leg carries this explicitly. Tests
Sequence risk
Returning markets matter less than WHEN money enters: a crash in year 1 of a lump-sum entry scars the whole compounding path. Stress tests
Cash drag
The return forgone while money waits in the parking leg — quantified at 4.5–6.6% of terminal value over 30y in no-crash scenarios. Trade-off
Phased path (this project)
Sale proceeds parked in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months, then bought into stocks monthly — the owner-requested crash-softening structure.
Black swan
A rare, high-impact regime (1929 deflation, 2008 twin asset crash, 1970s stagflation) that 50-year averages hide. Modeled separately from the baseline. Regimes
