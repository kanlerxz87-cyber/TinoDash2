General framework · Research snapshot: 25 November 2026

✳

01 / Situation

One home is expected to transfer through a trust in a future decade. A second home is already owned by the heir and has rental occupancy. Evaluate both together; do not assume both are inherited.

02 / Decision

Compare same-year sale, living, rental, living then sale, renting then sale, and sale with a Treasury-to-stock phase. Agree decision thresholds before receiving projections.

03 / Property & income tax

Confirm ownership and trust terms, date-of-death valuation, basis, assessment and Prop 19 eligibility/deadlines. Check rental depreciation, 121 eligibility, selling expenses and whether 1031 is viable before selling.

04 / Estate & liquidity

Inventory all assets and liabilities privately; review prior taxable gifts, portability and estate filing/payment. Determine cash needed, funding sources and a reserve. Federal basic exclusion differs by year.

05 / Missing evidence

Obtain independent property and rent estimates; bills, tax-rate area, insurance quotes, debt, leases, occupancy, capital plan and solar history. Do not treat unknowns as zero.

06 / Investment policy

Set asset allocation, reserves, time horizon, acceptable loss and rebalancing. Compare immediate investment to staging; weigh reinvestment and missed-rebound risk alongside crash protection.

Decisions to document privately: chosen path / date / assumptions / rationale / review date. Bring records securely; do not paste them into a public website.
