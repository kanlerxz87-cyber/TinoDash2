Attorney & CPA Briefing Sheet

Inheritance decision: one home expected through trust; a separate home is already owned and has reported rental/owner-occupancy use. Need a coordinated estate, property-tax, income-tax, landlord and investment analysis.

Evidence incomplete · no path selected

Purpose & household objective

Goal: maximize long-term after-tax household value while preserving required housing, adequate liquidity and acceptable risk. Confirm or amend goal before reviewing projections.

Written decision rules (date/sign offline): define the conditions selecting (1) live, (2) rent, (3) sell/reinvest, or (4) mixed; minimum cash reserve; risk limit; occupancy priority; acceptable annual operating burden; tie-breaker.

Two different properties · private identifiers omitted

Property A: California home expected to be inherited through a trust, estimated timing around 2040–2050. No address, ZIP, APN or exact financial figures included in this public copy.

Property B: already owned by the intended heir; reported multi-unit property with two current tenancies and another unit potentially available for owner use. Leases, legality, occupancy rights and net cash flow are unverified.

Immediate questions for attorney

  • Who holds title/beneficial ownership; what does the trust authorize and when does transfer occur?
  • Does the family relationship and transferor occupancy qualify for Prop 19? What claim, one-year residence deadline and value test apply?
  • Confirm death-date appraisal, transfer date, title change, assessment, Property A TRA/rate and supplemental bill process.
  • Estate liquidity, gross estate/adjusted taxable gifts, federal Form 706 and portability; prior gifts/706, spouse DSUE and deadlines.
  • For Property B: review every lease, unit legality, rent/eviction protections, owner occupancy and notice requirements.
  • Should a Section 1031 exchange be considered? Decide and obtain advice before disposition.

Immediate questions for CPA

  • Separate estate tax, income-tax basis and county assessment; document basis by asset/ownership share.
  • Model a sale in the inheritance tax year; then live-then-sell, rent-then-sell, keep/rent, and mixed ownership.
  • Calculate federal & California tax, capital gains, §121 eligibility, depreciation and potential recapture, rental allocation and suspended items.
  • Include debts, escrow/broker/transfer/recording charges, insurance, property tax, utilities, repair and five-year capital needs.
  • Estimate inheritance-year estate tax; current 2026 federal basic exclusion is $15 million; 2040–2050 limit is unknown.
  • Test range inputs, actual values and tax-aware sensitivity; do not carry today’s rate/threshold to a future year.

Deadlines & authority anchors

Form 706: generally due within 9 months of death when required; timely Form 706 can be needed for portability. Prop 19: eligible claimant generally must occupy as principal residence within one year and file applicable claim/exemption. Confirm exact date and county process. 2026 law only: federal basic estate exclusion $15,000,000; BOE Prop 19 allowance $1,044,586 applies only to transfers Feb. 16, 2025–Feb. 15, 2027. Do not project either amount to 2040–2050.
