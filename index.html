📋 Executive Summary — Important Details
Read this first; every number below is $10-rounded and sourced. Unverified cells are blank and flagged.

The Two Properties (Anonymized)

  • Property A — Cupertino 95014 (to be inherited via trust ~2040–2050): Nov 2026 FMV $3,100,000; 2026 assessed $550,540 (+2.0% BOE CCPI); parent tax ~$7,330/yr (2026 projected). Solar NEM credit −$180/yr; water $1,500/yr; insurance $1,950/yr primary / $2,450/yr landlord.
  • Property B — SF Outer Sunset 94122 (already owned): Nov 2026 FMV $1,800,000; 2025 assessed $114,250; 2025 tax $2,440; 3 units — Units 1–2 rented $7,150/mo ($85,800/yr), Unit 3 vacant/owner-occupied. Water $4,380/yr (3 units), electricity $600/yr, insurance $3,600/yr.
  • Key distinction: Property B is already owned — its low Prop 13 basis ($114,250) is an asset to protect. Moving to Cupertino and renting SF Unit 3 (~$3,500/mo) adds income; staying in SF keeps $7,150/mo + imputed rent.

The Three Tax Systems (Never Conflate)

  • Estate tax (IRC §2001/§2010): 2026 exclusion $15,000,000 per person (OBBBA §70106, Rev. Proc. 2025-32); $30M married with DSUE portability; 40% top rate. At 2045 baseline gross estate $16.9M → $0 tax (1-spouse exclusion ~$24.4M indexed).
  • Income-tax basis (IRC §1014): Heir gets stepped-up basis = FMV at death. Same-year sale → $0 capital gains. CA Form 540 taxes gains as ordinary income: 12.3% + 1% MHSA above $1M = 13.3% top rate (R&TC §17041). Delayed sale pays 13.3% + 25% §1250 recapture + 3.8% NIIT.
  • Property-tax assessment (Prop 13 / Prop 19 R&TC §63.2): Parent FBYV $801,960 at 2045 + $1,565,580 Prop 19 allowance = $2,367,540 ceiling. Occupy → $59,930/yr; Rent (no exclusion) → $78,470/yr. SCC ad valorem ~1.185% + fixed charges.

What the Model Says (2045 → 2075, 30-yr)

  • #1 Path 1A Same-Year Sale: $34,995,260 — wins because $0 tax + compounding beats 2.2% rental yield.
  • #2 Path 1B Phased Sale (30m Treasuries): $33,387,560 — costs $1.6M in bull market, saves $3M+ in crash.
  • #3 Path 4 Live-then-Sell (3 yrs, Prop 19 + §121): $33,261,500 — best if you want to live in Cupertino briefly.
  • #4 Path 5B Rent-then-1031 (7 yrs): $33,004,060 — defers recapture but suffers 7 yrs of full tax reset.
  • #5 Path 3 Occupy Long-Term: $29,166,730 — lifestyle choice, not wealth-maximizing.
  • #6 Path 2 Rent Long-Term: $24,643,810 hold / $17,757,090 liquidated — worst; Prop 19 reset eats ~50% of rent.

Before You Decide — Non-Negotiables

  • Obtain written DOD appraisal of Property A (sets §1014 basis).
  • Decide 1031 intent in writing before selling; get advice before listing.
  • Write the Investment Policy Statement (IPS) before money arrives.
  • Ask now about DSUE portability & prior taxable gifts (Form 706).
  • Claim Prop 19 only if you will occupy — file BOE-19-P + Homeowners' Exemption within 1 year.
