01 / THE DECISION

First, establish the facts.

One property may be inherited; a second is already owned. They are not the same transfer.

01

The inherited property

Residence, rental, sale, or a sequence of these. The transfer can trigger a new property-tax assessment.

02

The existing property

Already owned by the heir. Rental income and occupancy choices belong in every comparable path; inheritance alone does not transfer it.

03

The broader estate

Its assets, liabilities, prior gifts and available cash—not just the property—determine estate-tax exposure.
