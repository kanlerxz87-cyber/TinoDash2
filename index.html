What would change the answer — read this first.
  • Prop 19 occupancy: If the heir does not occupy Property A as a primary residence within 1 year of transfer, the parent-child exclusion is lost and Property A is reassessed to full market value (BOE). That single fact can swing annual tax by tens of thousands. Details
  • Same-year sale: Selling in the inheritance year (stepped-up basis, IRC §1014) ≈ $0 federal gain on pre-death appreciation. Waiting years adds post-death appreciation + depreciation recapture if rented. Paths
  • Market rent for A: No appraisal exists. Rent paths use a $3,800/mo median floor benchmark. If true market rent is $6,200/mo, the rent path jumps. F-04
  • Crash timing: Lump-sum stock entry the year before a 2008/1929-style crash loses 57–89%. The phased Treasury path (24–36 mo) softens entry but lags in bull markets (reinvestment risk). Black Swan
  • Estate size: Federal exemption is $15M/person (2026, permanent under OBBBA). Below that, estate tax = $0; above it, 40% on the excess. California has no estate/inheritance tax. Taxes

Executive summary

Under current law and base assumptions (3% home growth, 7% stocks, 4.3% Treasuries), no single path dominates across all horizons — see live ranking below. The model compares all paths at the same transfer date and horizon, with taxes, debt (none assumed — confirm), occupancy, capital costs, and liquidity. Mixed paths (live-then-rent, rent-then-sell) are included, not only pure choices.

This is a decision-support model, not tax/legal advice. Confirm with attorney + CPA before acting. See Briefing Sheet.

How to use this site

  1. Properties — the two houses, user tax tables (rounded), utility/insurance ranges.
  2. Projections — year-by-year market value, assessed value, tax, insurance, maintenance for 50 years.
  3. Paths Compared — live / rent / sell / mixed, same dates, with taxes, debt, occupancy, capital costs, liquidity.
  4. Black Swan — 1929/2008/1973/2022 stress tests + phased Treasury investing path.
  5. Taxes & Law — estate vs. income-basis vs. property-tax (separated), Prop 19, Form 540 1% tax, deadlines timeline, enacted vs. proposed.
  6. Verification — every row sourced to an official site or flagged blank. Flagged — irregularities for your review.
  7. Questions & Missing Info — what must be answered before any decision is reasonable, and how to discover it.
  8. Suggestions — improvement ideas; choose the best path before anything is implemented.
  9. Attorney & CPA Brief — one-page printable sheet.

Pre-agreed decision rule (anti-reverse-engineering): the household should agree in writing what would make it choose each path before relying on these figures. Template on Questions.
