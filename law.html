Important distinction: three separate tax systems

  • Estate tax: federal transfer tax measured on taxable estate and adjusted taxable gifts; not the same as income tax basis or county assessment.
  • Income-tax basis: controls gain/loss on sale and depreciation adjustment. Preserve appraisal, closing records and capital-improvement invoices.
  • Property-tax assessment: county assessed value used for property-tax billing; a transfer can trigger a change-in-ownership review, with Prop 19 rules potentially relevant.

Do not use one “tax value” as a substitute for these separate valuations.

San Francisco Prop H · preliminary result / terms

As of the Department of Elections update dated Nov. 23, 2026, preliminary results show YES 74.42%; the election page states results remain preliminary and may change until certified (deadline can be up to 30 days after Election Day). Do not describe it as finally certified until the official certification is posted.

The official ballot summary proposed a 15-year annual Muni parcel tax starting July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2042, indexed annually for inflation. Multifamily parcel base: $249 for up to 5,000 sq ft of building area, plus $0.195 per sq ft above 5,000, capped at $50,000 per parcel. The measure text includes exclusions/relief and a limited pass-through for qualifying rent-controlled units (up to 50%, capped at $65/unit); building area, unit classification, rent-control coverage and exemptions must be checked. These are nominal 2027 proposal rates, not a bill or confirmed Property B assessment. The Property B model now includes an explicit Prop H scenario control; its calculation remains blank until area, indexation and qualifying pass-through are verified.

Public data, privacy and source limitations

No property-specific public record search was run because parcel identifiers and exact addresses are intentionally excluded. To look up Property A’s Tax Rate Area and published rate, the owner or adviser should retrieve the secured tax bill / assessor record privately by APN, then match its TRA to the county’s official current rate publication. Do not paste an APN into this public website. Insurance premiums, rental comparables, values, utilities and capital needs cannot be validated from a general government law page; require written insurer, broker, assessor and contractor evidence.
