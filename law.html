Assessment and transaction details

For a qualifying transfer, the general assessment calculation starts with the existing factored base-year value; if market value exceeds that base plus the applicable allowance, the excess is added to the base. Subsequent inflation adjustments and continued eligibility must be checked; a move-out may cause reassessment. The transfer-year allowance is not the income-tax basis. '+link('boe')+'

Santa Clara County’s recorder describes a documentary transfer tax of $0.55 per $500 or fraction of value, subject to exemptions and details; recording fees depend on documents. Obtain a closing quote and do not infer a transaction-specific cost. '+link('transfer')+'

Proposition H: terms and status

The official voter text describes a multifamily base of $249 in the first year, plus $0.195 per square foot above 5,000 square feet, capped at $50,000, with adjustments and possible owner-occupied senior deductions. These statutory figures are not personal data; use full statutory precision, not rounded legal rates. Applicability, eligible building area, senior eligibility and certification are unverified.
