🧪 Phased-Investing Path (Path 1B) — How It Softens Crash Entry Risk
Park sale proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months after inheritance before phasing into stocks. CA-tax-exempt (31 U.S.C. §3124).
Mechanism: Day 1: 100% in 3M–2Y Treasury ladder (4.2% baseline yield, flight-to-safety rally in crashes). Months 1–30: phase 1/30th into equities monthly. If a crash hits in Year 1, only ~40% is exposed; the rest buys the dip. Cost in bull market: ~$1.6M over 30 years vs lump-sum. Benefit in crash: +$1.2M to +$3.9M by Year 10.

🏚️ 50-Year Non-Constant Regimes — Why Constant Growth Is Wrong
Baseline projections assume smooth 4%/7.8% growth. Reality is regime-switching.

Regime Playbook (What to Expect)

  • 1929-style deflation: Equities −60%+, CPI −24%, Treasuries positive real yield. Phased sale wins by $3.1M. Renting fails (tenants default, rents −30%).
  • 1973-style stagflation: Equities −28%, CPI 11–13%, Treasuries 9–15%. Real estate hedges inflation but Prop 13 (1978) caps tax growth at 2% — holding wins on real basis. Phased sale still wins nominally.
  • 2000-style lost decade: Equities flat for 10 yrs, SCC rents −15–20%. Phased sale +$1.2M advantage. Occupy path suffers vacancy.
  • 2008-style housing crash: Equities −35%, Bay Area RE −18–27%. Selling Day 1 at peak locks in FMV; holding marks down. Phased sale +$3.9M.
  • M7.0 quake: Uninsured loss 25–35% + 18-mo uninhabitable. Diversified portfolio −5.8% max. Insurance (CEA 15% deductible) is not optional if you hold.

How to Use This Page

  • Pick the regime you fear most. If 1929/2008 keeps you up at night, choose Path 1B (36m) and write it into the IPS before inheritance.
  • Re-run this analysis annually with updated inputs (see Governance).
  • Track proposed legislation that could change regimes (Prop 19 repeal, wealth tax) — see Enacted vs Proposed.
  • Do not reverse-engineer the path from one projection. Agree in writing what would make you choose each path before figures arrive.
