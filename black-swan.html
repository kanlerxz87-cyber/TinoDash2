Black swan — real-world stress conditions

Growth and inflation are never consistent over 50 years. This section mimics real conditions: crashes, stagflation, housing drawdowns, and rate shocks. Baseline projections assume smoothness; reality does not.

Stress 1 · Stagflation (1973-style)

Inflation 8% + stocks −30% + home growth 1% for 5 yrs. Rent caps (5%+CPI, max 10%) bind; property tax still +2%/yr (Prop 13). Live path (imputed rent) is relatively sheltered; leveraged rent path suffers.

Stress 2 · Housing crash (−30% A value)

A falls 30% post-inheritance. Sell path locks in pre-crash price (if sold early); live/rent paths carry mark-to-market loss + higher tax-to-value ratio (Prop 8 decline-in-value review may help — ask assessor).

Stress 3 · Rate shock (Treasuries 1%)

Parking yield collapses. Phased advantage shrinks; lump-sum wins. Test by lowering stock return on Dashboard — if ranking flips, the answer is rate-sensitive.

Stress 4 · Longevity + low returns (5% stocks, 1% homes)

Low scenario on Dashboard. Rent path (yield + appreciation) vs. sell path (5% compounding) — the gap narrows; liquidity of sell path matters more.
