What must be answered before a reasonable decision?

This is the decision-readiness register. The “current answer” column only repeats general case facts supplied in conversation, not independently verified. No sensitive amounts or address details are exposed here. All household-specific answers should live in a private estate file.

First gate: answer the household’s non-financial rules before receiving a projection: what does “best” mean (after-tax wealth, liquidity, stable housing, workload, income or flexibility), which options are unacceptable, and what evidence would make the household switch paths? Sign/date that policy before comparing results.

Does California or another jurisdiction impose estate/inheritance/transfer tax in the actual year?Current California State Controller guidance: no California estate-tax return is required for decedents dying after Dec. 31, 2004; another state’s domicile and local transaction/recording obligations are unresolved.Confirm domicile, property situs, trust/ownership, any multi-state assets, current law and transfer charges with counsel in the year of death.Do not conflate state death tax, estate income tax, property assessment and recorder/transfer tax.
