DUE DILIGENCE / 02

The questions
before the answer.

Known: one home is expected to transfer, while the other is already owned. Everything property-specific below remains private and unverified here.

QuestionWhat must be establishedHow to find outSource
Eligibility & trustNot verified in this public guide.Who inherits, when, and under which trust terms? Was the inherited property the transferor’s principal residence? Trustee and attorney: review trust, title, occupancy and authority. Do not publish documents.Official source ↗
Independent valuationNot verified in this public guide.What are the date-of-death value, land/building allocation and credible rent? Obtain a written appraisal and multiple local rental comparables. Voucher schedules are not a market appraisal.Official source ↗
Taxes & assessmentNot verified in this public guide.What are the parcel’s tax-rate area, local rates, fixed charges, adjusted base and eligibility? Privately consult the assessor and current tax bill; do not infer a parcel rate from a ZIP code.Official source ↗
Estate exposureNot verified in this public guide.What is the full estate, adjusted taxable gifts, marital status, DSUE and cash available for taxes? Create a secure inventory with the attorney and CPA. Determine filing, portability and liquidity needs.Official source ↗
Existing propertyNot verified in this public guide.What are each lease, permitted unit status, occupancy rights and lawful rents? Attorney checks leases, rent and eviction rules; preserve actual operating history and verify occupancy.Official source ↗
Operating costsNot verified in this public guide.What are policy premiums, utility statements, solar credits, maintenance and repair needs? Get insurance quotes for the correct occupancy and coverage; inspect both buildings and prepare five-year work plans.Official source ↗
Basis & dispositionNot verified in this public guide.What is the tax basis and adjusted rental basis; is an exchange or home-sale exclusion possible? Obtain allocation and depreciation records. Discuss exchange eligibility and intermediary before contracting to sell.Official source ↗
Portfolio policyNot verified in this public guide.What reserves and downside can the household tolerate; what asset mix and tax budget apply? Write a policy for staging, rebalancing, spending, reinvestment and review before funds arrive.Official source ↗
Do not reverse-engineer the choice.Before valuation arrives, write down what conditions would make each path acceptable. Record the decision and date in the private planning file—not this public site.
