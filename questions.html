Questions, information & answers — what must be addressed before any decision

No decision is reasonable until the blocking items are answered. Each question states what is known, what is missing, and exactly how to discover it. Personal information stays internal — use placeholders on this site.

Pre-agreement (anti-reverse-engineering)

Agree in writing, before relying on figures, what would make the household choose each path. Fill, date, sign, file:

If the figures later contradict this agreement, the agreement governs — or is formally amended with a new date. This prevents reverse-engineering the outcome from one projection.

Answers already established (from official sources)

  • CA estate/inheritance tax = $0. Federal exemption $15M/person (2026). → Taxes.
  • Prop 19 requires occupancy within 1 year; rentals get no exclusion. → Taxes.
  • Same-year sale ≈ $0 gain (step-up). → Paths.
  • CA 1% tax applies only above $1M; gains are ordinary income. → Taxes.
  • Rent growth capped at 10% (state) and possibly lower (SF). → Taxes.
