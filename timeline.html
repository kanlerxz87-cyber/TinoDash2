THE PROCESS / 03

A timeline for
the long view.

Relative deadlines are guides, not automatic calendar dates. Counsel should determine the legally relevant transfer date and all filing requirements.

Now

Secure trust, estate inventory, prior gift records and a dated decision policy; obtain independent insurance, property and rental estimates.

Official source ↗
At death / transfer

Trustee confirms authority, secures assets, obtains written date-of-death appraisal and identifies creditors, cash needs and the change-in-ownership date.

Official source ↗
Within 9 months

If required, generally file Form 706 and pay estate tax. A filing extension is not automatically an extension to pay; ask about portability.

Official source ↗
Within 1 year

For eligible family-home relief, claim the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption and establish qualifying occupancy; confirm exact deadlines with the assessor.

Official source ↗
Within 3 years or before transfer

Generally claim the parent-child exclusion by the earlier of these dates for timely relief; special late-claim rules may apply.

Official source ↗
Before a sale

Compare sale in the transfer tax year with other paths; obtain basis, selling costs and professional advice on any like-kind exchange before closing.

Official source ↗
Every year

Refresh law, rents, insurance, valuations, reserves and shock scenarios; rebuild the private model and record whether the recommendation changed.
