Clock discipline

Three clocks start at death: the estate-tax return (9 months), the Prop 19 homeowners' exemption (1 year), and the exclusion claim (3 years). Missing any one can cost five to six figures.

05 · PROCESS, STEP BY STEP

How the inheritance would actually unfold

The transfer is expected by trust in the 2040–2050 window. Steps below are keyed to the law as published on Nov 25, 2026 — re-verify every window at the actual date. Recheck list

Phase 0 — Now, before anything transfers (2026 →)

NOW · PRIVATE

Build the private estate inventory

Full-precision record of assets, trusts, beneficiary designations, debts, insurance, and prior taxable gifts — kept in git-ignored private/, never published. Privacy policy

NOW · ADVISERS

Agree the decision criteria in writing

Before the final figures exist, record what evidence would favor each path (done — see the flip thresholds) and have the attorney/CPA review them. Flip thresholds · Briefing sheet

NOW · MARKET

Order the evidence the model is missing

Date-of-death valuation plan, independent rental comps for Property A, insurance quotes for both buildings, lease review for Property B, five-year inspections. Question list

EVERY NOVEMBER

Annual recheck + model rebuild

Re-verify federal exclusion, Prop 19 allowance window, FTB line, county rate book, SF parcel taxes, and legislation; rebuild the model and record whether the recommendation moved. Rebuild protocol

Phase 1 — At the transfer (trust administration)

DAY 0 · TRUSTEE

Trustee takes control; notices and inventory

Trustee (or executor) secures the property, insurance, and records; statutory notices to beneficiaries. Brief the attorney

DAYS 0–90 · VALUATION

Obtain written date-of-death valuation of Property A

Licensed appraisal at date of death (not listing value); retain comps and method. This number sets the stepped-up basis and the Prop 19 math. Basis rules

MONTHS 0–9 · FEDERAL

Form 706 if required; portability election

Due 9 months after death (+6-month extension via Form 4768). File even if no tax is due when portability matters; consistent-basis statements to beneficiaries. Form 706 instructions

MONTHS 0–12 · PROP 19

Prop 19 filings — if and only if occupying

Homeowners' (or disabled veterans') exemption application within 1 year of transfer; exclusion claim within 3 years. Claiming without genuine long-term occupancy is improper and risky. BOE Prop 19

YEAR 1 · COUNTY

Reassessment and supplemental bill

County assessor enrolls the new base value (with Prop 19 cap if claimed); supplemental assessment covers the partial year. Budget it. Supplemental rules

Phase 2 — Execute the chosen path (months 0–36)

MONTH 0 · MODEL

Re-run the model with REAL figures

Replace planning values with the actual valuation, verified rents, quotes, and the estate total; confirm the ranking did not move. Protocol

MONTH 0–2 · IF SELLING

Same-tax-year sale sequence

List with negotiated commission; county transfer tax and recording at close; gain ≈$0 via step-up; proceeds to the investment policy. Cost stack

MONTH 0–36 · IF PHASING

Treasury parking + scheduled phase-in

Park in 3M–2Y Treasuries (Nov 2026 curve: 3M 4.34%, 2Y 4.61%); buy equities monthly over 24–36 months; roll maturities; log each reinvestment yield. Tests · Treasury rates

MONTH 0–24 · IF LIVING

Occupancy + insurance switch + B management

Move in (starts the 2-of-5-year §121 clock), owner-occupied insurance policy, decide Property B's open unit. Occupancy matrix

CONTINUOUS · POLICY

Written investing policy governs the money

Allocation bands, emergency fund, phase schedule, rebalancing rule, no speculation, annual review — agreed before proceeds arrive. Policy draft

Phase 3 — Ongoing (annual rhythm)

ANNUAL · NOVEMBER

Extreme-scenario review + model re-run

Re-test 1929/2008 entries against the current portfolio; refresh regime anchors; confirm insurance/capex plans. Black swan section

ANNUAL · JUNE 1

San Francisco applications (if at B)

Prop H senior (65+) exemption or adjustment applications due June 1 for the upcoming fiscal bill; Rent Board anniversary and AB 1482 caps on rent increases. Prop H terms · Rent Board

BIENNIAL · BOE

Prop 19 allowance re-adjustment watch

BOE re-sets the allowance every two years by FHFA California House Price Index — affects the live-in math if the transfer has not happened yet. Current adjustment
