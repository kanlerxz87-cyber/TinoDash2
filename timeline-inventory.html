🛤️ Step-by-Step Inheritance & Filing Deadlines Timeline
Trust inheritance ~2040–2050. All deadlines are statutory — miss one and you lose the benefit.
Before Death (Now)
Write the IPS & Pre-Commitment Governance

Agree in writing what would make you choose each path before figures arrive. Write the Investment Policy Statement for sale proceeds. Ask about DSUE portability & prior taxable gifts. Obtain independent Property A appraisal & rental estimates. See Governance.

Day 0 — Date of Death
Obtain Written DOD Valuation of Property A

Sets IRC §1014 stepped-up basis = FMV at death. Without it, basis is unproven and same-year sale tax benefit is lost. Order qualified appraisal immediately.

Within 60 Days
Secure Property, Notify Insurer & County

Change locks, notify homeowners insurer (vacancy), notify SCC Assessor of trust transfer. Start probate/trust administration. Verify Property B leases (SF Rent Ordinance).

Within 9 Months
Federal Estate Tax Return (Form 706) — If Required

Due 9 months after death if gross estate > exclusion ($15M in 2026, indexed). File for DSUE portability even if no tax due. Pay any tax with estate liquidity plan (see below).

Within 1 Year
Prop 19 Deadline — Occupy + BOE-19-P + Homeowners' Exemption

Claim Prop 19 only if heir will occupy long-term. Must occupy as principal residence + file Homeowners' Exemption + Form BOE-19-P within 1 year of transfer. Late filing = full reassessment ($78,470/yr vs $59,930/yr). BOE Prop 19

Same Tax Year as Inheritance
Model a Sale in the Same Tax Year — Before Deciding Anything Else

Same-year sale = $0 capital gains (IRC §1014). Waiting a year creates taxable gain + CA 13.3% tax. Decide 1031 intent before listing; get advice before selling.

45 / 180 Days (If 1031)
IRC §1031 Like-Kind Exchange Windows

45 days to identify replacement property via Qualified Intermediary; 180 days to close. CA taxes the exchange (FTB Form 3840). Miss the window = taxable sale + §1250 recapture.

Annually
Re-Run Model, Review Extreme Scenarios & Track Legislation

Rebuild scripts/build_model.py with updated inputs; check whether recommendation moved. Review Black Swan regimes. Track Prop 19 repeal / wealth tax proposals.
