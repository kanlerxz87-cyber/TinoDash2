## Important source boundaries

- 2026 federal and current 2025 state tax references cannot determine liability in 2040–2050.
- An official general page cannot establish a private person's relationship, occupancy, basis, local rent, parcel tax rate area, insurance eligibility, legal unit count, or property condition.
- Public historical anchors (DJIA 1929–32 price decline; S&P 500 nominal calendar years 1931/1973/1974/2000–02/2008/2020; bear-decade CAGRs 1930s/2000s) are published on `black-swan.html` with sources and index-basis labels. No household-specific 1929/2008 replay is published; one requires an adviser-agreed total-return source, allocation, dividends, fees, rebalance rule, dates and inflation deflator.
- Proposals must remain labelled proposed until official enactment and effective dates are verified.
