Different paths.
One fair comparison.

Establish decision rules before looking at projections. Until inputs are verified, this is a comparison framework—not a recommendation.

THE FAIR-COMPARISON RULE

Same clock, same household.

Use identical transfer dates, horizons and inflation assumptions for every path. Include both properties, debt and payoff, taxes, insurance, utilities, capital work, vacancy, transactions, liquid reserves, investment contributions and imputed rent. Count housing benefits once.

For living then selling, assess the actual two-of-five-year ownership and use tests, previous exclusions and rental-use rules—not an automatic switch. Official source ↗

WRITE THIS DOWN FIRST

Precommit to the rules.

Choose minimum liquid reserves, maximum acceptable downside, time willing to live in the home, landlord workload, risk limits and the minimum advantage required to justify a change. Record the decision, date and rationale privately. Revisit annually.
