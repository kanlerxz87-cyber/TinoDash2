Paths compared — same transfer dates, same horizons

Six paths, identical inheritance year and horizon. Taxes, debt, occupancy changes, capital costs, and liquidity are shown for each. Debt: none assumed (confirm — Q-01). Liquidity: sell paths convert A to cash/Treasuries/stocks; live/rent paths keep wealth in illiquid property.

1 · Live in A (move from B)

Heir occupies A within 1 year → Prop 19 exclusion preserved (capped). B stays 2 rented + 1 owner unit. Imputed rent = avoided market rent (input on Dashboard). §121 exclusion available on later sale (2 of 5 yrs). Prop 19: eligible

2 · Rent A, stay in B

A rented at market (floor $3,800/mo). Prop 19 exclusion lost (no occupancy) → reassessed to market. Depreciation 27.5-yr + unrecaptured §1250 (≤25%) on sale. Vacancy 5% + mgmt 8% haircuts. Prop 19: lost

3 · Sell A immediately (same tax year)

Stepped-up basis ≈ sale price → ≈$0 federal gain on pre-death appreciation (IRC §1014). Net proceeds → Treasury parking → phased stocks. Transfer tax + recording priced in (sell cost 6.5%). Lowest sale tax

4 · Live-then-sell (5 yrs)

Live 5 yrs (Prop 19 + §121 $500k joint exclusion), then sell. Post-death appreciation partially sheltered. Best if heir wants Cupertino long-term.

5 · Rent-then-sell (10 yrs)

Rent 10 yrs (depreciation accumulates), then sell. Recapture cost grows with holding period. Tests whether rent yield beats immediate reinvestment.

6 · Live-then-rent (5 + 15 yrs)

Mixed path: live 5 yrs, then rent. Captures Prop 19 + §121 history, then rental yield. Shows why mixed paths matter — pure choices hide this.

Like-kind exchange (1031) — decide before selling

§1031 defers gain on investment property exchanged for like-kind property. It does not apply to a primary residence, and it is unnecessary if stepped-up basis already zeroes the gain (immediate sale). Take advice before selling — 1031 has 45/180-day deadlines. Flagged as Q-06. Model does not assume 1031; if 1031 is wanted, the sell path must be re-run as an exchange path.
