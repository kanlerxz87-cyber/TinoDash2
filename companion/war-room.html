DECISION COCKPIT · NO HOUSEHOLD FIGURES · NOT ADVICE

The decision is not ready. Here is exactly what is missing.

Nine gates stand between the current evidence and a defensible choice between living in, renting, selling, or splitting the inherited home. Below them is the pre-commitment register that should be signed before any appraisal, rent opinion or projection is ordered.

!
No defensible winner yet — by design.

Four of the nine gates below are missing outright and the rest are unverified. Any number produced before those gates close would be a precision the evidence cannot support. What this site can do today is fix the decision rules, price the public-law anchors, and show how the staged-investing idea performs against real history.

01 · FIRST SCREEN

Do not decide until these are answered

Each gate changes the ranking of the options, not merely the size of the numbers.

02 · PRE-COMMITMENT

Write the choice rule before the figures arrive

Agreeing the conditions in advance is the only real defence against reverse-engineering a decision from one projection. Fill this in, date it, and only then order appraisals and rent opinions.

If we choose…We commit to this evidence and condition firstWe change our mind if…Where it is recorded
We will live in the inherited homeDocumented, durable occupancy intent; a written statement that the home will be the primary residence within one year and remain so; acceptance of the concentration and capital obligations.Occupancy is convenient rather than intended, or the household would move within a few years.Journal · RTC 63.2
We will rent it outIndependent market rent that clears all costs after tax with a vacancy and management allowance; a lawful, insurable rental structure; a tax plan for depreciation and recapture.Evidenced rent does not cover carrying costs plus a reserve, or the legal/insurance structure is unavailable.Comparison · IRS Pub 527
We will sell and reinvestWritten investment policy before proceeds arrive; a tax estimate for a same-tax-year sale; a reserve sized to near-term needs; a decision on phased versus immediate deployment.Net proceeds cannot fund the stated reserve, or the household cannot tolerate the equity risk the plan requires.Crash replay · Treasury bills
We will keep a mixed positionAn explicit split, a reason for each part, a separate cost and tax treatment for each, and a review date.The split is a way of postponing the decision rather than expressing a preference.Mixed paths · IRS Pub 523
