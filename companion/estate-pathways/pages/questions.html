WHAT WE KNOW · WHAT WE DO NOT · HOW TO FIND IT

Questions, information and answers needed before any decision

A decision is only as good as its evidence. Below is the register: each item shows what is known today, what is missing, and the suggested discovery step. Suggested discovery steps are the "how to find missing information" plan — none of these require publishing private data anywhere.

3 · Answers we can already give (with sources)

QuestionAnswer todaySource
Is there a federal inheritance tax on each heir?No — the federal tax is an estate tax on the estate, above the exclusion; heirs pay income tax only on post-death growth.IRS estate tax
Does California levy estate or inheritance tax?Not for deaths on/after Jan 1, 2005 under the current framework.CA State Controller
What is the estate-tax threshold for 2026?$15,000,000 per person; $30,000,000 married with portability.IRS
Is inherited property's capital-gain basis the old assessed value?No — basis steps up (or down) to FMV at death; assessed value is a different system.IRS FAQ
Does Prop 19 apply automatically on inheritance?No — it requires a qualifying family-home transfer and the transferee's principal residence and a filed claim.BOE
Can the family-home exclusion carry to a later replacement home?Yes, Prop 19's portable base-year provisions allow carry-forward to a replacement principal residence within the statutory period — confirm rules then in force.BOE Prop 19
