DEADLINES AS A SEQUENCE · TRUST TRANSFER 2040–2050

How the inheritance process looks, step by step

A trust transfer usually avoids probate, but it does not avoid deadlines. Exact dates depend on the trust instrument and the law at the death/transfer date — treat this as the checklist, not the calendar.

  1. Now → transfer (2040–2050 window)

    Prepare before figures arrive

    Write the decision criteria and the investment policy; ask the trustee/executor about prior taxable gifts and portability; keep a private estate inventory; obtain independent valuation and rental-estimate methodology; verify Prop 19 eligibility conditions; review Property B leases and local rules; re-run this model annually.

    Owner: household + estate attorney / CPA
  2. Date of death / trust transfer

    Authority, title, valuation

    Trustee confirms the certification of trust, title, debts, beneficiaries; commission a written date-of-death valuation (income-tax basis = FMV at death, IRC 1014); secure insurance and utilities continuity; do not publicly disclose the inventory.

    Owner: trustee
  3. Within 1 year of transfer

    Prop 19 occupancy + homeowners' exemption

    Only if genuinely occupying Property A long term: establish the homeowners' exemption (statutory filing within one year; limited relief under SB 293 for specified 2025 wildfire cases). Claiming without occupying risks escape assessments. Official terms

    Deadline: 1 year (BOE) · Owner: heir + county assessor
  4. Within 3 years of transfer (or before any third-party sale)

    Prop 19 exclusion claim (BOE-19-P)

    File the parent-child exclusion claim; late claims get a six-month window after a supplemental/escape assessment notice. BOE forms

    Deadline: 3 years (R&T 63.2) · Owner: heir
  5. Same tax year as the inheritance

    Price the same-year sale FIRST

    CPA computes the sale in the inheritance tax year (step-up ⇒ ≈$0 gain); escrow prices transfer tax and recording fees; decide §1031 only with advice before any disposition. Sale stack

    Owner: CPA + title/escrow
  6. 9 months after death (+6-month extension)

    Federal Form 706 (if required / for portability)

    Required if the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts exceeds the year-of-death exclusion; consider filing anyway to elect portability for a surviving spouse. No California estate return under current law. Estate ledger

    Deadline: IRS 9 months · Owner: executor + CPA
  7. Year of death + following April

    Income-tax filings

    Final Form 1040/540 of the decedent; fiduciary income returns if the trust has income; report any sale (Schedule D; CA treats gain as ordinary income; line 62 1% over $1,000,000 taxable income). FTB booklet

    Owner: CPA
  8. Fall 2027 (Property B, ongoing)

    SF Prop H parcel tax begins

    First on the fall 2027 secured bill; parcel-area lookup and applications open Dec 1, 2026; exemption/adjustment requests due Jun 1, 2027. Terms

    Owner: Property B owner
  9. First year after transfer → annually

    Stabilize and re-run

    Confirm recorded title and assessor record; review any supplemental assessment and appeal windows; document occupancy; renew insurance; validate leases; re-run the model with updated inputs and re-check the law watch; write the decision and date in the decision log. Extreme-scenario review annually (replays).

    Owner: household + advisers
