AGREE THE RULES BEFORE THE FIGURES ARRIVE

Decision criteria, investment policy, and the decision log

Reverse-engineering a decision from one projection is how households pick the wrong path. Write down what evidence would favour each path — dated and signed — before any appraisal or sale figures arrive. Then compare the figures against the written rules, not against feelings.
