Attorney & CPA Briefing Sheet

Objective: decide — on written criteria agreed in advance — whether to live in, rent, sell (immediately or staged), or blend paths for Property A (inherited via trust ≈2040–2050, Santa Clara County), while Property B (San Francisco, 3 units / 2 rented ≈$7,150/mo) stays a separate, already-owned asset.

Key figures (rounded, planning only)

  • Property A est. value ≈$3,100,000 (2026); assessed ≈$550,540 (history ≈1.33% effective rate).
  • Same-year sale stack ≈$2,908,590 net on $3,100,000 (6% friction + 0.11% DTT + ≈$2,000 fees; ≈$0 gain tax via step-up).
  • 2026 federal exclusion $15,000,000 / $30,000,000 (portability); CA has no estate return (deaths ≥2005).
  • Prop 19 cap = factored base + $1,044,586 (transfers 2/16/2025–2/15/2027); next adj. Feb 2027; 2040–50 amount unknown.
  • CA 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax above $1,000,000 taxable income (Form 540 line 62, not indexed).
  • Model (2045/10y/base): sell+invest ≈$11,003,480 · live-in ≈$9,275,580 · rent ≈$8,669,140.

Questions for the attorney

  • Trust administration: trustee powers to sell/lease, beneficiary consent, title/debt, any constraints.
  • Prop 19: is the heir an eligible transferee; was A the transferor's principal residence; occupancy plan; filing clocks (1 yr / 3 yr / BOE-19-P).
  • Property B: lease legality, unit count (DBI), rent-control coverage, owner-occupancy effects, Prop H pass-through rules.
  • §1031: wanted or not — decide before any sale, with exchange counsel.
  • Portability & prior taxable gifts: is a Form 706 advisable at the first death?

Questions for the CPA / tax adviser

  • Written date-of-death valuation; consistent-basis reporting (§6035) if Form 706 filed.
  • Compute the same-tax-year sale first; then 1/5/10-year sale cases incl. §121 (only if genuine occupancy) and recapture on any rental depreciation (25% federal + CA ordinary).
  • CA gain = ordinary income; model line 62 (1% over $1,000,000) in the sale year.
  • Estate tax exposure given total estate, gifts, portability; liquidity plan if above the exclusion.
  • Reassessment outcomes: Prop 19 claim vs full reassessment; supplemental assessments; appeal windows.

Inputs to bring to the meeting

  • Appraisal(s) + rental comparables; tax bills; TRA/direct-assessment printouts (keep parcel IDs off shared copies).
  • Insurance quotes (both buildings); utility history; five-year inspection reports and bids.
  • Leases and registration records (B); trust instrument; prior gift/estate filings.
  • Signed decision criteria + investment policy (pages/decision-criteria.html).
Decision guardrail: the criteria were agreed before the figures; revisit only with a written, dated reason. Current law cannot be projected as certain for 2040–2050. Anchors: IRS Form 706/1014/121 · CA BOE Prop 19 + LTA 2025/009 · FTB Form 540 · SFMTA Prop H · SF Rent Board.
