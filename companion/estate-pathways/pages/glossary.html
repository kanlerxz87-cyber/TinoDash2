TERMS THAT SHOULD NOT BE MIXED UP

Glossary

Three tax systems

Estate tax
Federal tax on a deceased person's estate, above the basic exclusion ($15,000,000 in 2026). Not an inheritance tax on each heir.
Income-tax basis
Starting point for capital-gain math on a sale. Inherited property is generally stepped to fair market value at death (IRC 1014) — not the old assessed value.
Assessed value / factored base-year value
California property-tax value under Proposition 13: rises at most 2%/yr until a reassessment event. Not market value, not basis.

California property tax

Proposition 13
1978 constitutional limits: ~1% general levy, 2% annual assessment cap, reassessment on change of ownership/new construction.
Proposition 19 family-home exclusion
Parent-to-child (and qualifying grandparent) transfer of a principal residence can avoid reassessment up to a value cap ($1,044,586 adjustment for 2025–2027 transfers). Requires transferee occupancy and a filed claim.
Carry-forward (portable base year)
Prop 19 lets the parent's base-year value (and remaining cap headroom) move to a replacement principal residence within the statutory period.
Tax rate area (TRA)
Zone with a common rate stack (general levy + voter debt + direct assessments). The parcel's bill depends on its TRA — city averages cannot identify it.
Documentary transfer tax
County tax on deeds ($0.55 per $500 = 0.11% in Santa Clara County). SF has a separate, tiered city transfer tax.
Supplemental / escape assessment
Corrective bills after ownership changes or errors; they also start claim-filing clocks.

Sale & investment

IRC §121 primary-residence exclusion
$250,000/$500,000 gain exclusion after 2 of the last 5 years of ownership and use as a principal residence.
Depreciation recapture (§1250)
Rental depreciation reduces basis; on sale, depreciation taken (or allowable) is taxed up to 25% federally, plus CA ordinary rates.
§1031 like-kind exchange
Tax-deferred swap of business/investment property. Not available for a personal residence by default; decide before selling.
Imputed rent
The market-rent value of living in a home you own, minus carrying costs. Economic benefit, not cash received.
Reinvestment risk
Maturing short-term securities must be reinvested at whatever yield exists then. The premium of the phase-in plan's crash protection.
Cash drag
Return given up by holding cash/Treasuries while risk assets rise — the cost side of staging.
Step-up in basis
Reset of basis to date-of-death FMV; the reason the same-tax-year sale shows ≈$0 gain.
Portability (DSUE)
A surviving spouse can use the deceased spouse's unused federal exclusion — only if a timely Form 706 elected it.

Economics

Nominal vs real return
Nominal ignores inflation; real = nominal minus inflation effects. Over 50 years the difference compounds brutally.
Black swan (this site's use)
Rare, severe market regimes used as entry tests (1929, 1973–74, 2008, 2022) — not forecasts.
Fair Market Rent (FMR)
HUD's program benchmark for voucher payments. A floor/anchor for rent modelling — never a market appraisal.
Behavioral Health Services Tax
California's 1% additional tax on taxable income over $1,000,000 (Form 540 line 62). Formerly "Mental Health Services Tax."
