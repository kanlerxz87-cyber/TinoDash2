Write household decision rules before the projections. Define each path’s trigger, non-negotiables, minimum reserve, max occupancy disruption and acceptable investment decline. Sign/date it; revise only with a reason recorded.Highest priority · reduces hindsight bias / choosing to fit one forecast. No investment result is promised.SEC Investor.gov diversification overview ↗
Commission independent valuations. Date-of-death appraisal for basis, current as-is appraisal/opinion and separate written market-rent analysis for the inherited home; obtain a second opinion if the result is pivotal.Before any tax or rent ranking · valuation and net rent are material and not independently known.IRS inherited basis reference ↗; HUD FMR (not a market comp) ↗
Ask the county assessor and estate attorney to pre-review Prop 19 eligibility. Check transferor residence, relationship, trust transfer timing, claimant occupancy, principal-residence exemption, value-limit and required claim documents.Before the one-year window begins · can materially alter carrying costs; do not claim without long-term occupancy intent.California BOE Prop 19 guide ↗
Build an encrypted private estate inventory. Include all real/personal assets, ownership, date-of-death value evidence, debts, lifetime gifts, insurance, accounts and key documents. Keep an access log; publish none of it.Before estate-liquidity estimate · necessary for taxable-estate analysis and liquidity, but must remain outside the public GitHub repository.IRS estate tax information ↗
Have SF housing counsel review every lease, unit status and proposed owner occupancy. Verify building age, registration, rent/eviction protections, permits, owner move-in rules and state-law coverage.Before assuming rent or occupancy · one vacant unit does not by itself prove lawful use or right to displace a tenant.SF Rent Board / city rules overview ↗
Prepare a five-year maintenance and capital plan for both properties. Obtain property inspections and written cost bands for major systems; add recurring repair, vacancy, insurance renewal and contingency reserves.Before hold-vs-sell ranking · keeps one-time capital costs and recurring operations visible and separate.Cupertino building resources ↗; SF Building Inspection ↗
Adopt a written investment policy before sale proceeds arrive. Establish liquidity bucket, allocation, diversified benchmark, fees, rebalancing, withdrawal plan, risk limits, staging/rate roll assumptions and authorized decision-makers.Before investing · test immediate and staged strategies at the same date/horizon; explicitly price the reinvestment and inflation risk of short Treasuries.TreasuryDirect bill terms ↗; SEC Investor.gov ↗
Commission tax-aware sensitivities. Ask CPA to model same-year sale, later sale after occupancy, rental, mixed occupancy, depreciation/recapture, §121, California capital-gain treatment, Prop 19 and sale costs—each with low/base/high evidence-backed inputs.Before ranking · only CPA can resolve personal return facts and future-law assumptions. Keep estate tax, income-tax basis and assessment as separate model layers.FTB capital-gain instructions ↗; IRS depreciation guide ↗
Track legislation in two separate lists: proposed and enacted/effective. Include official bill number/status, source, date checked, operative date, affected assumption and who rechecks it.Annual plus before transfer/sale · prevents proposal from being silently treated as current law.California Legislative Information ↗; Congress.gov ↗
Review extreme scenarios annually. Include multiple-year inflation, prolonged equity drawdown, rate rollover after the Treasury hold, long vacancy/repair, insurance nonrenewal, tax-law changes and estate liquidity stress.Annual · one crash year is not a realistic 1929/2008 replay and smooth 50-year growth is not reliable.Treasury rollover mechanics ↗; SEC risk/diversification ↗
